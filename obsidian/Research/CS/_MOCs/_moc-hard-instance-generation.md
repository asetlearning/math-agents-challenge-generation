# Hard Instance Generation — Map of Content

**A reading path for generating hard problem instances that come with a known solution or a certificate**, for benchmarking and training search algorithms. It is cross-domain: random CSP/SAT, ML instance generators, and group theory (trivial words, Andrews–Curtis). It was built for [[project-challenge-gen]]. Start with [[_synthesis-hard-instance-generation]].

---

## Why random instances are easy: generic-case and average-case hardness
- [[kapovich-2003-generic-case-complexity]]: on generic inputs, the word problem is solvable in linear time, even in groups where it is undecidable.
- [[krzakala-zdeborova-2009-quiet-planting]]: where the hard region lies in planted random CSPs.

## Certified generation by random walks / moves from a known solution
- [[miasnikov-1999-ac-genetic]]: random AC walks from the trivial presentation. A cautionary result: scrambling a hard seed is undone by greedy search
- [[shehper-2024-ac-hardness]]: about 1.8M certified presentations from reverse walks on Miller–Schupp seeds
- [[elder-2015-random-trivial-words]]: a Metropolis chain whose trajectory is the certificate (a product of conjugates)

## Group theory: trivial words
- [[elder-2015-random-trivial-words]]: Metropolis sampling of trivial words
- [[2607.26241]]: WPNet, a GNN embedding classifier for word equality and geodesic length. It trains on certified trivial words generated by "tangling" (at most 150 insertions): a certified generator **without** hardness control, i.e. a generic baseline
- [[coulon-2018-trivial-elements-criterion]]: triviality in B(r,n) is equivalent to the existence of a sequence of power-removing moves. This holds only for large odd n, so it does **not** apply to B(2,5); it shows the shape a certificate takes

## Group theory: Andrews–Curtis
- [[andrews-curtis-conjecture]]: the problem
- [[miasnikov-1999-ac-genetic]]: genetic-algorithm search for AC-trivialisations
- [[shehper-2024-ac-hardness]]: RL on AC, with a hardness measure
- [[carreras-2026-ac-certificates]]: machine-checkable AC-equivalence certificates
