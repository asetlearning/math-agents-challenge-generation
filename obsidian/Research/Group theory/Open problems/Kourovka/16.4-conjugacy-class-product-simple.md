# Statement

Let (G) be a finite group with (C,D) two nontrivial conjugacy classes such that (CD) is also a conjugacy class. Can (G) be a non-abelian simple group?

# Why selected

The question is finite and concrete. A positive answer is a finite witness check; a negative answer can be attacked by character-table and class-product constraints, with GAP's character-table library providing a reproducible first screen over finite simple groups.

# Deliverables

Re-read the statement from the source PDF, check current literature/status, search available finite simple-group character tables, and distinguish a bounded exclusion from a proof for all finite simple groups.
