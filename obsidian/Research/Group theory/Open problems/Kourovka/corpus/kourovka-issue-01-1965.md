*Issue 1 (1965) · printed p. 6 · proposed by L. Ya. Kulikov*

Suppose that G is a finitely presented group, F a free group whose rank is equal to the minimal number of generators of G, with a fixed homomorphism of F onto G with kernel N . Find a complete system of invariants of the factor-group of N by the commutator subgroup [F, N ].

## 1.74

*Issue 1 (1965) · printed p. 6 · proposed by V. P. Platonov*

Describe all minimal topological groups, that is, non-discrete groups all of whose closed subgroups are discrete. The minimal locally compact groups can be described without much effort. At the same time, the problem is probably complicated in the general case.
