## 18.1

*Issue 18 (2014) · printed p. 115 · proposed by I. M. Isaacs*

Given a group G of finite order n, does there necessarily exist a bijection f from G onto a cyclic group of order n such that for each element x ∈ G, the order of x divides the order of f (x)? An affirmative answer in the case where G is solvable was given by F. Ladisch.

## 18.12

*Issue 18 (2014) · printed p. 116 · proposed by V. G. Bardakov*

Let G be a finitely generated group of intermediate growth. Is it true that there is a positive integer m such that every element of the derived subgroup G′ is a product of at most m commutators? This assertion is valid for groups of polynomial growth, since they are almost nilpotent by Gromov’s theorem. On the other hand, there are groups of exponential growth (for example, free groups) for which this assertion is not true.

## 18.24

*Issue 18 (2014) · printed p. 117 · proposed by M. Burger, D. Calegari*

For a group G, a function φ : G → R is a quasimorphism if there is a least non-negative number D(φ) (called the defect ) such that |φ(gh) − φ(g) − φ(h)| ⩽ D(φ) for all g, h ∈ G. A quasimorphism is homogeneous if in addition φ(g n ) = nφ(g) for all g ∈ G. Let φ be a homogeneous quasimorphism of a free group F . For any quasimorphism ψ of F (not required to be homogeneous) with |φ − ψ| < const, we must have D(ψ) ⩾ D(φ)/2. Is it true that there is some ψ with D(ψ) = D(φ)/2?

## 18.35

*Issue 18 (2014) · printed p. 118 · proposed by A. Gaglione, D. Spellman*

A family F of group homomorphisms A → B is separating if for every nontrivial a ∈ A there is f ∈ F such that f (a) 6= 1, and discriminating if for any finitely many nontrivial elements a1 , . . . , an ∈ A there is f ∈ F such that f (ai ) 6= 1 for all i = 1, . . . , n. Let G be a relatively free group of rank 2 in the variety of metabelian groups. Let T be a metabelian group in which the centralizer of every nontrivial element is abelian. If T admits a separating family of surjective homomorphisms T → G must it also admit a discriminating family of surjective homomorphisms T → G? A positive answer would give a metabelian analogue of a classical theorem of B. Baumslag.

## 18.36

*Issue 18 (2014) · printed p. 118 · proposed by J. D. Dixon*

Univ.), 5 (2005), 39–43 (Russian)). One condition which might characterize subgroups of S is the following. Consider the metric d on S where for all x 6= y we define d(x, y) := 2−k when k is the least element of the set N such that k x 6= k y . Then (S, d) is a separable topological group, and so each subgroup of S (not necessarily a closed subgroup of (S, d)) is a separable topological group under the induced metric. Is it true that every group G for which there is a metric d′ such that (G, d′ ) is a separable topological group is (abstractly) embeddable in S? Note that any such group G can be embedded as a section in S.

## 18.42

*Issue 18 (2014) · printed p. 119 · proposed by D. Calegari, A. Walker*
