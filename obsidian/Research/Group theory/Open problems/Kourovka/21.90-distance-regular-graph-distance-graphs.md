# Kourovka Problem 21.90 — Distance Regular Graph Distance Graphs

## Source statement

> 21.90. Let Γ be a graph of diameter d. For i ∈ {1, 2, . . . , d}, let Γi be the graph on the same vertex set as Γ with vertices u, w adjacent in Γi if and only if dΓ (u, w) = i. Does there exist a Q-polynomial distance-regular graph Γ of diameter 3 such that Γ2 and Γ3 are strongly regular? A. A. Makhnëv
