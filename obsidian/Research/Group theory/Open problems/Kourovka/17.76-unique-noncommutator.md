# Statement

Does there exist a finite group (G), with (|G|>2), such that exactly one element of (G) is not a commutator?

# Why selected

This is a finite-witness existence question. A positive answer can be certified by an explicit multiplication-table or permutation-group witness; a negative answer may be approachable through commutator-width and character-theoretic constraints. The first cycle should test small groups and isolate necessary structural conditions, not claim a universal theorem from bounded computation.

# Deliverables

Record the source-PDF transcription and staleness check, search a bounded catalogue with exact outputs, and state whether a concrete witness or a mathematically live obstruction emerges.
