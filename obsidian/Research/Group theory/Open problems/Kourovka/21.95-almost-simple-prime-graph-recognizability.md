# Kourovka Problem 21.95 — Almost Simple Prime Graph Recognizability

## Source statement

> 21.95. Is there an almost simple but not simple group which is recognizable by the isomorphism type of its Gruenberg–Kegel graph? Note that if a group G is recognizable by the isomorphism type of its Gruenberg– Kegel graph, then G is recognizable by its Gruenberg–Kegel graph, and therefore G is almost simple (P. J. Cameron, N. V. Maslova, J. Algebra, 607 (2022), 186–213). N. V. Maslova
