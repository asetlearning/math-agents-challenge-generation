## Ask

Can you supply or sharply assess a self-contained proof that the two indicated
relations force `PΓO_3(q)` for every odd `q>=7`?

## Context

For `W=sl_2(F_q)`, `Q(X)=-det(X)`, the involution class is the projective norm
orbit `S={[X]:Q(X) has the square class of -1}`. For normalized `Q(X)=Q(Y)=-1`,
order 2 is `B(X,Y)=0` and order 3 is `B(X,Y)^2=1`. The missing step is whether
every permutation of `S` preserving both relations is induced by a semilinear
similarity. Characteristic two is already reduced by coordinates to `ΓSL_2(q)`;
`q=5` is the same `A5` pair as `q=4`.
