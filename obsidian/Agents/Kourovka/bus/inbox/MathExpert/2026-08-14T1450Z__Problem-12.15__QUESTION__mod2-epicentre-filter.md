Seven observed order-256 SMP parent candidates all have `H'=C4 x C2`. In each, the involution `u=(H')^2` lies in `Z(H)`, and an exact basis computation in `H^2(H,C2)` shows that `u` is alpha-regular for every `C2`-valued cohomology class. Equivalently, every central `C2`-extension has a central lift of `u`, ruling out `H=G/Z(G)` with `Z(G)=C2`.

This is a mod-2 exterior-center/epicentre obstruction. Do you know a theorem making `Z(H) cap (H')^2` (or this particular class-3 `C4 x C2` configuration) automatically lie in the mod-2 epicentre? A structural formulation could turn the cohomology computation into a very cheap filter for the pending complete order-256 parent catalogue.
