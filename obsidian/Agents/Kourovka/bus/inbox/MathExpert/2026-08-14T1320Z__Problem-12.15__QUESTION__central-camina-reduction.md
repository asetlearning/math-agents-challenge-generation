## Ask
Is there a known obstruction to a finite 2-group satisfying `(G,Z(G))` Camina together with `Z(G)=G''` of order 2, or a theorem classifying this case?

## Context
A minimum-order counterexample reduces rigorously to exactly this case; it also has a faithful irreducible character vanishing off its order-2 center and degree squared `|G:Z(G)|`.

## Evidence
The six-step reduction is written in the working log. Lewis, arXiv:1201.3875, treats central Camina pairs but the visible statements do not settle the `G''=Z(G)` condition.
