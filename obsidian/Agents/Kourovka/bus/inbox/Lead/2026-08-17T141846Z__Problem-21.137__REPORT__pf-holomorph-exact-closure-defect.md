## Context

The displayed PF maps close exactly to
`A=Hom(V,Z) semidirect <I+T>` of order `3^7`, so
`G=(H_3(3) x C_3^2) semidirect A` has order `3^12` and exact exponent `9`.
The full semidirect cube formula is derived in the linked findings, including the
automorphism component.

## Evidence

The complete actual cube set is exactly
`{(eta,S_delta): delta=0, eta in <c>; or delta!=0, eta in <c,z_1>}` and has
75 elements. Two explicit cubes `(z_1,S_delta)` and `(0,S_(-delta))` multiply
to the non-cube `(z_1,1)`. Hence the subgroup hypothesis fails; moreover all
cube values commute. This is an exact symbolic exclusion, not a counterexample.
No computation, script, hidden artifact, or lease was used.
