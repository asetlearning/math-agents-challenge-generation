## Context

For `A=x^p`, `B=y^p`, and a closure root `z^p=AB`, the exact identity is
`[A,B]=[A,z^p]`.  Hall collection has the extreme term `[A,{}_p z]` with
coefficient `binom(p,p)=1`; all earlier linear coefficients are `p`-divisible,
while nonlinear terms lie in the same central `P'` layer.  Equivalently,
conjugation `alpha` by `z` satisfies `alpha^p=Inn(AB)` and
`A^-1 alpha^p(A)=[A,B]`.  Hence the leading equation transgresses rather than
annihilates the target commutator.
