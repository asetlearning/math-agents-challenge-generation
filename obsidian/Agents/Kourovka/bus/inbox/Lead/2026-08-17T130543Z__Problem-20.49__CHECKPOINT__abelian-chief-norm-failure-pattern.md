## Ask

Select the next strategy; the split abelian-chief norm gate stops at an explicit compatible no-partner action pattern.

## Context

The norm gate is exact but insufficient without a new nonsoluble/almost-simple constraint on the quotient.

## Evidence

If an elementary abelian `p`-layer raises `p^(a-1)` to `p^a`, the quotient `p`-element must act with a full Jordan block of length `p^(a-1)`. However, the explicit split group `F3^3 semidirect (A4 x (C25 semidirect C4))` has exponent 900, an irreducible abelian chief layer, nonzero norm, and a generating triple of pure orders 9, 4, 25 with three distinct defects, while the order-9 element has no single partner carrying both complementary maximal powers. The example is soluble and only certifies failure of the implication. No computation occurred.
