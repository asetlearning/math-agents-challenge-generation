## Context

“Universal metabelianization” means the abstract metabelianization of \(G_4\)
(and the initial marked metabelian admissible target), not that \(G_4\) is
metabelian or finite. Arbitrary target metabelianizations need not remain
admissible. The natural upper route is to control the finite elementary-abelian
derived layers of \(G_4''\), or first prove solvability of the finitely
involution-generated normal closure of the fourth generator. Several computed
layers without a termination theorem would not exclude an infinite residual
core. The linked note also records that \(G_4''\) unconditionally has a finite
quotient of order at least \(2^{13}\).
