## Exact action-level exclusion

The closed lift reduction gives one demand label and capacity at most `27` per centrally regular outer element, regardless of `k`: `n_u(abar)=9` for one `abar` or zero, the source capacity is `9k`, and the demand cap is `27`.

For each derived central branch, the number of centrally regular elements in `U` is forced by the computed branch itself:

- cyclic regular image `C3`: `2|U|/3`;
- elementary centralizer image `C3^2`: `6|U|/9=2|U|/3`;
- full `UT3(3)`: `12|U|/27=4|U|/9`.

Thus:

- if `|U|=3`, there are only two possible admitted elements, so max flow is at most `2*27=54<216`;
- if `|U|=9`, either eligible branch has exactly six centrally regular elements, so max flow is at most `6*27=162<216`.

Therefore every permitted action image of order 3 or 9 fails the factor-independent capacity gate, for both quotient types and without full-outer fusion or an epimorphism calculation.

Only images of order at least 27 remain:

- for `C3^4`: embedded `C3^3` or `C3^4`;
- for `H_3(3)xC3`: embedded `C3^3`, `H_3(3)`, or `H_3(3)xC3`.

This is conditional action-level narrowing of the order-`3^9` equality family, not a counterexample or quotient-type elimination. The unrestricted scope remains unanswered.
