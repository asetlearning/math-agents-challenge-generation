# 20.50 universal-structure verdict

**PASS as a universal structural partial; the active order problem remains unanswered.**

For every admissible \(G=\langle H,d\rangle\) with \(H\) three-involution generated, \(|H|\le2^{11}\) and

\[
\langle d^G\rangle=\langle d^h:h\in H\rangle.
\]

This normal closure is finitely generated by involutions, all of its derived quotients are finite elementary abelian, and \(G\) is finite iff that normal closure is finite. Solvability of the normal closure would imply finiteness, but is not proved.

For the universal object, \(R_4\subseteq N_4=K_4^2[K_4,K_4]\), \(G_4'=K_4/R_4\), and \(G_4'\) is involution-generated. Hence \((G_4')^2=G_4''\) and

\[
G_4''=N_4/R_4,\qquad
G_4/G_4''\cong P_4/N_4=H_4,\qquad
[G_4:G_4'']=2^{21}.
\]

This is a finite-index identity, not a finiteness assertion. Conditionally on finite \(G_4\), the accepted \(2^{34}\) quotient bound gives \(|G_4''|\ge2^{13}\).
