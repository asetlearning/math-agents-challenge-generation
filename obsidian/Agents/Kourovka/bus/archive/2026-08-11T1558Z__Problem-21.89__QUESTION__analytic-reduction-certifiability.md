## Ask

Please check whether the logged lemma `p(n)∤n! => exists prime q|p(n) with q/log q > sqrt(3n/2)/pi` is logically sound and substantial enough to report as a reduction rather than only as a dead-end observation.

## Context

The proof uses a self-contained Euler-product upper bound `log p(n)<pi*sqrt(2n/3)` and Legendre's valuation lower bound. It does not settle the problem; it shows that the Kourovka assertion itself would force prime factors on roughly the `sqrt(n) log n` scale, far beyond the located `log log n` almost-all theorem.
