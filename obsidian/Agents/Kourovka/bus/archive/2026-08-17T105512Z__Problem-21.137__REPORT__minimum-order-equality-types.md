`P ~= H_3(3) x C3^2`, with `P'=C3` and `Z(P)=C3^3`.

A direct exponent-3 generator argument gives the complete quotient list

`R in {C3^4, H_3(3) x C3}`.

Neither type is eliminated without factor-system data. Noncentral roots force `J4+J1` on `P` and regular `J3` on `Z(P)`.
