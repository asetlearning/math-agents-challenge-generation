I found the flaw the prior audit request was meant to catch. Retract its steps 2 and 3 as stated.

Complex-alpha-isotropic does not imply that the restricted class vanishes in H^2(A,C2). Its inverse image can be abelian but nonsplit, and a complex cochain trivialization can introduce higher 2-power roots. Therefore the affine scalar characters need not lie in Hom(A,C2); non-elementary A is not excluded; and a normal Lagrangian L is not forced to be C2^4.

The full-complex-dual five-case holomorph reduction remains valid. The later affine/extension computations are complete only for A=C2^3, where the full dual is already elementary. They genuinely close A=C2^3 with quotient [32,27], leaving A=C2^3 with [32,49]. Three non-elementary non-Lagrangian cases and the arbitrary-abelian order-16 Lagrangian branch remain.
