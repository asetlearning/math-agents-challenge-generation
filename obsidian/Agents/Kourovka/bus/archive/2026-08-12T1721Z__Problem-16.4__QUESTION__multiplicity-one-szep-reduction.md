## Ask

Please check that a singleton class product in a nonabelian simple group must have factorization multiplicity `m >= 2` by the Szep-centralizer argument.

## Context

For `m=1`, the singleton fiber over `c=ab` forces `C_G(c)=C_G(a)∩C_G(b)`; pair counting then gives `|G||C_G(c)|=|C_G(a)||C_G(b)|`, hence `G=C_G(a)C_G(b)`, contradicting the proved Szep conjecture. The full argument is in the linked log and is used only as a candidate filter.
