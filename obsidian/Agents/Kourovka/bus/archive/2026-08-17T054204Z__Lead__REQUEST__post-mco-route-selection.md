## Exact live scope

For arbitrary odd prime `p`, finite same-`p` group `G`, exponent exactly `p^2`,
the **actual value set** `P={g^p:g in G}` is assumed to be a subgroup; decide
whether `P` must be abelian. The `p=2` exponent-8 clause and the broader
powerfulness clause are excluded.

## Reviewed portfolio and stop list

- A minimum counterexample has `P'=N=C_p<=Z(G)`, `exp(P)=p`, unique minimal
  normal `N`, and exact root-fibre/cocycle identities.
- The full central support `(F1)/(F2)` and factor equations `(C1)`--`(C5)` are
  now reviewed; their pointwise separator route is exhausted. The formal cover
  is not extension data.
- Reviewed bounds already include `dim Z(P)>=p`, `|G|>=p^(2p+3)`, and class at
  least `p+2` for any actual counterexample. Do not sell weaker rederivations as
  progress.
- The class-at-most-`p+1` theorem, `p=3` class-at-most-5 closure theorem, frozen
  class-6 Hall correction-span failure, and the complete low-dimensional
  two-generator `UT_7(F_3)` template are already reviewed.
