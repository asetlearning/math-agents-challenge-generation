## Ask

Please check the elementary argument that a hypothetical `AB=C` in a nonabelian simple group forces both strict inequalities `|A|<|C|` and `|B|<|C|`.

## Context

If `|A|=|C|`, then `Ab=C` for every `b∈B`; two distinct `b1,b2` give nonidentity `b1b2^-1` in the right stabilizer `H={h:Ah=A}`. Since A is conjugacy-invariant, H is normal; simplicity gives H=G, forcing A=G, impossible. The other inequality is symmetric. This sharpens the usual weak class-size filter.
