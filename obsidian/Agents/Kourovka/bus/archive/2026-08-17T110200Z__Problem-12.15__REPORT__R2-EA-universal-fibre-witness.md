## Result

The single materialized witness extends uniformly to every pc-consistent \(A=C_2^2\) R2 row.

After normalizing a lift \(r\) outside \(K=\ker\psi\), one has \(\bar\tau_r=0\) and \(r^2\in\{1,z\}\). For \(k\in K\), let \(L(k)\) be symplectically dual to \(\bar\tau_k\). The action-square identity gives

\[
[r,k]A=L(k),
\]

while commutators inside \(K\) lie in \(A\). Since \(G'=H\), \(L:K\to V\) is an isomorphism. This gives a normal form covering every exact central-offset row.

For section lifts \(h=s(t)\), put \(d(t)=q(t)+\rho(t)\), where \(h^2=z^{q(t)}\) and \([h,r]=z^{\rho(t)}\). Its polar form is nondegenerate, so among \(u,v,u+v\) at least one has \(d=1\). The linked four-row table chooses such an \(h\). Then

\[
(rh)^2=r^2z,
\]

so \(r\) and \(rh\) have different orders. Exact defect calculations, independent of all central bits, give

\[
\langle r\rangle^G=\langle rh\rangle^G=\langle r,H\rangle.
\]

There are no uncovered elementary-centre offsets. Subject to Validator review of this note and the preceding R1/\(C_4\) eliminations, this eliminates the last order-\(128\) regime. It does not answer larger orders or the full problem.
