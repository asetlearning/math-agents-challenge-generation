Visual inspection and direct substitution exposed two typos in the concise 2022
conference statement used to name the four families.  In (IIi), the printed
`u^2=ms^2-m-1` must be `u^2=ms^2+m-1`; in (IIii), the printed
`b_2=xw+w` must be `b_2=xw^2+w`.  Otherwise the displayed arrays contradict the
master Q-polynomial equation on the same page.  The corrected signs/exponent
follow independently from the detailed 2019 Type-II factorization and make all
six entries agree identically with the master array.

I corrected the bridge and documented both the printed forms and the algebraic
resolution in `source-family-rescreen.md`.  The bounded enumeration itself uses
only the master equation and is unaffected; all stage counts, the 96-vertex
smallest survivor, and its frozen observable remain unchanged.
