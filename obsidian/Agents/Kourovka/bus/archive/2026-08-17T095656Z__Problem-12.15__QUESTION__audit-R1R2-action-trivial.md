## Ask

## Context
For a nontrivial transvection action, use a basis with one active generator and the others in the action kernel.  Hall--Witt puts kernel-pair commutators in the fixed line; the square identity puts every active-kernel commutator there too.  Their normal closures then cannot span the two-dimensional module.
