## Exact active scope

- `p` is odd and `p>2`.
- `G` is a finite same-`p` group of exponent exactly `p^2`.
- `P={g^p:g in G}` is the literal set of actual `p`th powers, never merely the
  subgroup generated by those powers.
- The hypothesis is that this literal set itself is a subgroup.
- The question is whether that subgroup must be abelian.
- The separate `p=2`, exponent-eight sibling is excluded.

The bounded lane below specializes to `p=3`; it does not answer other primes or
the universal assignment unless a proof explicitly bridges them.

## Frozen bounded target

Keep `K=3_+^(1+4) x C3^2` and quotient `Q=H_3(3)`, but require a genuinely
nonsplit extension. Reuse only the independently reviewed canonical nonabelian
symplectic outer action from the split seed, with matrices `A,B,C=[A,B]` on
`V=K/Z(K)`. This differs from the old NS3 route, which used the rank-two kernel
`H_3(3) x C3^2` and quotient `H_3(3) x C3`, and from MIN9.

Fix one nonorthogonal candidate cube-label pair, initially labels in the fixed
Lagrangians of `A` and `B`; derive and verify the exact coordinates and signs.
Solve over `F3` the full compatible lift system for
`alpha_X=(A,T_X,L_X)`, `alpha_Y=(B,T_Y,L_Y)`, and
`alpha_Z=(C,T_Z,L_Z)`, including:

1. `alpha_X^3=Inn(q_X)` and `alpha_Y^3=Inn(q_Y)`, with
   `[q_X,q_Y] != 1`;
2. the outer commutator relation matching `H_3(3)`;
3. `[alpha_X,alpha_Z]` and `[alpha_Y,alpha_Z]` inner;
4. every cube-label invariance and compatibility row required by the quotient
   relations.

The first gate is complete linear action/lift feasibility. If inconsistent,
provide an exact row-reduced rank/inconsistency certificate excluding this one
canonical nonorthogonal-lift row. If feasible, only then freeze a complete
nonsplit presentation/factor system and solve every consistency equation.

A counterexample claim additionally requires independent, exact checks of group
finiteness/order, exponent exactly nine, the complete literal cube set, subgroup
closure of that literal set, and two noncommuting actual cubes. A formal action,
cover, power-generated subgroup, or partial coset sample is never a witness.

No broad action, cocycle, or catalogue search; no alternate kernel or quotient;
no split-family reuse; no `p=2`. Heavy computation requires a frozen manifest
and explicit Lead lease.

## Lead hint to audit, not assume

For an automorphism `(M,T,L)` with `M=I+N`, `T=I+R`, and `N^2=0`, the reviewed
rank-four cube-inner equation is expected to reduce to
`R^2 L + R L N = J_q`. Individually, a label in `ker N` may be realizable (for
example by a choice resembling `x=J_q,y=0` in regular-nilpotent center
coordinates). Verify this from definitions. The real obstruction or opportunity
should lie in simultaneous cross-action and extension coherence.
