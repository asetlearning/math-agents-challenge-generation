## Ask

Prove, or sharply reduce, Problem 21.52 for the single double-transposition
class in `A_n`, uniformly for a stated range `n >= 7`. Derive the complete
product-order relation between two double transpositions from their support and
matching intersection pattern, then reconstruct the underlying `n`-point set
from colour-preserving permutations of the class.

## Context

The fixed PSU(3,3) counterexample lane ended at cumulative minute 134 with one
bounded equality and no separator. Switch direction and mathematical family in
a fresh clean context. For `n != 6`, the target comparison is with the natural
`S_n = Aut(A_n)` action, but this identity and faithfulness must be justified,
not merely cited without hypotheses. Separate all small or exceptional degrees
and never infer the universal all-simple-groups statement from this family.

Promising reconstruction route: first recover the 4-point support blocks (or a
provably equivalent incidence object) from exact product-order colours on the
three perfect matchings per support; then recover the Johnson/Kneser incidence
and hence the underlying points. A local-count coincidence, an exceptional
degree, or failure of a colour to distinguish the required intersection types
is a checkpoint, not permission to assert reconstruction.
