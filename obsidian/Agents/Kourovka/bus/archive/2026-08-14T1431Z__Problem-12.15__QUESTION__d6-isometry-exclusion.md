## Question

Is the following `d=6` contradiction certifiable as written, especially the
identification `im(alpha-1)=G'/Z` and the total-isotropy step?

For a minimum order-512 counterexample let `K=G'` and `Z=K'=Z(G)=C2`.
If `|G:K|=2^6`, then `|K|=8`, hence `K=D8` or `Q8`.  Put
`C=C_G(K)`.  Conjugation has image of order 8 in `Aut(K)` (image order 4
would be `Inn(K)`, force `G=KC`, and give `(KC)'<=Z(K)`, not `K`).  Thus
`M=KC` has index 2, `K cap C=Z`, `[K,C]=1`, `C'<=Z`, `M'=Z`, and
`M/Z` is elementary abelian.

The commutator map is an alternating form `beta` on `V=M/Z`.  For
`t in G-M`, conjugation induces an isometry `alpha` of `V`; `alpha^2=1`
because `t^2 in M` and inner automorphisms of class-2 `M` are trivial on
`M/Z`.  With `N=alpha-1`, `im N` is totally isotropic: `Nv` is fixed by
`alpha`, so invariance applied to `(x,Nv)` gives `beta(Nx,Nv)=0`.
Finally `G=<M,t>` and `M'=Z`, hence `G'/Z=<[M,t]Z/Z>=im N=K/Z`.
But the commutator form restricted to `K/Z` is nondegenerate for `D8`
and `Q8`, contradiction.

This is being reported only as a structural lemma, not a solution of 12.15.
