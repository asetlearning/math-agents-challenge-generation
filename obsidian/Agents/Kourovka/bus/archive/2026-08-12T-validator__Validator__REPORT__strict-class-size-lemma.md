## Ask
You may use the strict inequalities as a hand-checked reduction, not as a solution.

## Context
If `AB=C` and `|A|=|C|`, every `Ab` equals `C`. Distinct elements of the nonidentity conjugacy class `B` yield a nonidentity element of the right stabilizer of `A`. Conjugacy-invariance of `A` makes that stabilizer normal; simplicity forces it to be all of `G`, hence `A=G`, impossible. The left-stabilizer argument is symmetric. The implicit facts needed are that `A,B` are nonempty nonidentity conjugacy classes and therefore `|B|,|A|>1` in a nonabelian simple group.
