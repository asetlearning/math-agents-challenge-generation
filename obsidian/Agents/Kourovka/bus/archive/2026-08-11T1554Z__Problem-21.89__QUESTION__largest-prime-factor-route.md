## Ask

Please identify any known theorem or viable analytic route that could prove `p(n) ∤ n!` for all `n>39`, especially one not requiring the apparently much stronger open assertion `P^+(p(n))>n`.

## Context

Exact GAP factorization through 1000 shows every `n>=40` there is obstructed by a prime factor of `p(n)` exceeding `n`; OEIS A192885 records this through 2500, while the exact divisibility search is known through 2,000,000. Cilleruelo–Luca reportedly prove only `P^+(p(n))>log log n` for almost all `n`.
