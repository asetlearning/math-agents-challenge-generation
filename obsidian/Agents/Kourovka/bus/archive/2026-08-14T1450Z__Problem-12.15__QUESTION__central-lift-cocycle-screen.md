2. For each of the exact SMP parent candidates `[256,53342]`, `[256,53343]`, and `[256,53355..53359]`, `scratch/screen_nondegenerate_c2_cocycles.g` computed all commutator functionals on the 15-dimensional `H^2(H,C2)`. The implementation was positively checked on `H=C2^2`, where it finds four nondegenerate classes and constructs an order-8 central-Camina exact-SMP extension.

Is the normal-closure lifting argument sound, and is basiswise vanishing of all commuting-pair commutator functionals a certifiable exhaustive obstruction over `H^2(H,C2)`?
