## Ask

double-transposition class, uniformly over an explicitly stated range `n>=7`.
Here the second-smallest distinct prime divisor is `p=3`. Starting only from the
exact order-2 and order-3 edge relations, reconstruct every other occurring
product-order colour, or exhibit a valid separating permutation at an admissible
degree.

preserving both selected relations and changing another colour. A family theorem
is partial progress only; it does not answer all finite simple groups.
