# Named approach

Nonabelian factor-set classification for \(Q_8\)-by-\((C_4\times V_4)\) extensions with an order-three lift.

# Why this family is first

The equivariant template is excluded through order 64, so order 128 is the first unscreened order and its common kernel has order eight. Math Expert's current-inbox audit recommends genuinely noncentral/nonabelian extension data. The quaternion group \(Q_8\) is the order-eight kernel with a natural order-three outer symmetry.

# Outer-action reduction

Use
\[
\operatorname{Aut}(Q_8)\cong S_4,
\qquad
\operatorname{Inn}(Q_8)\cong V_4,
\qquad
\operatorname{Out}(Q_8)\cong S_3.
\]
The quotient action and the desired order-three lift give a homomorphism
\[
C_4\times A_4\longrightarrow \operatorname{Out}(Q_8)\cong S_3.
\]
The \(A_4\)-image is either trivial or \(C_3\): these are the only quotients of \(A_4\) that embed in \(S_3\). If it is \(C_3\), the commuting \(C_4\)-image is a 2-subgroup of \(C_{S_3}(C_3)=C_3\), hence is trivial. If the \(A_4\)-image is trivial, the \(C_4\)-image is trivial or \(C_2\). Thus there are exactly three outer-action types:

1. \(A_4\twoheadrightarrow C_3\), with outer-trivial \(C_4\);
2. outer-trivial \(A_4\), with \(C_4\twoheadrightarrow C_2\);
3. both factors outer-trivial.

# Exact factor-set data

For each outer action \(\bar\rho:Q=C_4\times V_4\to\operatorname{Out}(Q_8)\):

1. choose lifts \(\rho_q\in\operatorname{Aut}(Q_8)\);
2. choose factors \(c(q,r)\in Q_8\) satisfying
   \[
   \rho_q\rho_r=\operatorname{Inn}(c(q,r))\rho_{qr};
   \]
3. impose the twisted associativity equation for \(c\);
4. impose invariance, up to a 1-cochain, under the automorphism fixing \(C_4\) and cycling the nonzero elements of \(V_4\), and require an actual lift of exact order three.

Equivalently, check the obstruction in \(H^3(Q,Z(Q_8))\), then enumerate the resulting torsor under \(H^2(Q,Z(Q_8))\), where \(Z(Q_8)\cong C_2\). Every surviving factor set gives an exact multiplication table on \(Q_8\times Q\), hence a group of order 128.

Every automorphism of \(Q_8\) fixes its unique central involution, so the induced \(Q\)-action on \(Z(Q_8)\cong C_2\) is trivial. For
\[
Q=C_4\times C_2\times C_2,
\]
the standard decomposition
\[
H^2(Q,C_2)\cong \operatorname{Ext}^1(Q,C_2)\oplus
\operatorname{Hom}(\textstyle\bigwedge^2 Q,C_2)
\]
has dimension \(3+\binom32=6\) over \(\mathbb F_2\). Hence every unobstructed outer action has exactly 64 cohomology classes before quotienting by compatible automorphisms or imposing the order-three lift. Across the three outer-action types, at most 192 classes reach those later filters.

The quotient 3-cycle reduces this further. Write \(x\) for the degree-one class from \(C_4\), and \(y,z\) for those from \(V_4\). A degree-two basis may be taken as
\[
u,\ y^2,\ yz,\ z^2,\ xy,\ xz,
\]
where \(u\) generates \(H^2(C_4,C_2)\). The 3-cycle fixes \(u\); on \(\langle y^2,yz,z^2\rangle\) its fixed line is generated by \(y^2+yz+z^2\); and it has no fixed vector on \(\langle xy,xz\rangle\). Therefore
\[
\dim H^2(Q,C_2)^{\tau}=2.
\]
For any unobstructed outer action, the extension classes form an affine torsor under this cohomology group. Its \(\tau\)-fixed set is empty or a coset of the two-dimensional fixed subspace, hence has at most four elements. Across all three outer-action types, at most 12 extension classes survive quotient-level order-three invariance, before requiring an actual lift of cube one or checking the two preimages.

# Tests and certificate

Form the coordinate preimages \(K/N=1\times V_4\) and \(L/N=C_4\times1\). Compare center, derived subgroup, Frattini quotient, exponent, element-order counts, and lower-central data before attempting an explicit isomorphism. A survivor must output the full multiplication/factor table, order-three automorphism, subgroup generators, and a relation-checked isomorphism \(K\to L\).

# Kill criterion and limits

Exhaust the obstruction and at most four invariant factor-set classes for each of the three outer actions. A negative result excludes only the \(N\cong Q_8\), \(|P|=128\) family. Reassess \(D_8\) and the abelian order-eight kernels before increasing \(|P|\). Any implementation or algebra-system run requires a fresh exact Lead lease.
