## Subclaims

1. For even `q`, `SL(2,q)=PSL(2,q)` is simple for `q>=4`, and has exactly one
   involution class represented without omission or duplication by nonzero
   vectors.
2. For the proposed matrices `u_x`, dependence of `x,y` is exactly product order
   2 for distinct vertices, while determinant one is exactly product order 3.
3. A permutation of nonzero vectors preserving dependence and determinant-one
   pairs is precisely determinant-one semilinear; all exceptional small fields
   must be covered without using nonexistent graph edges such as `(x,x)` or a
   zero vector.
4. The same semilinear maps preserve every exact product order, proving both
   inclusions in the bounded equality.
5. The notation `PGammaL(2,q)` agrees with the semilinear group obtained in even
   characteristic.
6. `PSL(2,5)` and `PSL(2,4)` give the identical abstract `A5` pair, including the
   chosen complete involution class and both minimal primes.
7. The odd-characteristic rigidity lemma and the finite `q=27` context are not
   used; no other simple group is covered.
