## Target versus witness

- Source target object: every finite group and every admissible character/element
  pair.
- Witness actually computed in: the stored CTblLib ordinary character table with
  identifier `3.U3(5)`.
- `witness = universal target`: false; the witness is one member of the quantified
  class, not the target class itself.
- The narrower identity `CharacterTable("3.U3(5)") =` the ordinary table of the
  simply connected group `SU_3(5)` is supported in the submission by CTblLib
  metadata (`2A`, `sc`, `l=2`, `q=5`, construction `SU(3,5)`, centre `C3`, simple
  quotient `U3(5)`) and remains to be reconstructed independently.

## Subclaims

1. The frozen artifacts have the reported hashes and provenance, including a
   one-command Lead lease.
2. CTblLib resolves the identifier to the ordinary character table of the
   simply connected finite group `SU_3(5)`, distinct from `U3(5)` and `U3(5).3`.
3. The table has order 378000, 40 classes, 40 irreducible rows, the reported
   class orders and degrees, and exact cyclotomic values.
4. The transcript contains exactly one logical record for each of the 1,600
   row/class coordinates and its stored fields are internally arithmetically
   consistent.
5. Exactly 987 table values are nonzero in exact cyclotomic arithmetic.
6. Every one of those 987 cells has zero integer remainder modulo
   `degree * class_order`.
7. This establishes only a bounded zero-hit result for `3.U3(5)` and leaves the
   universal assignment unanswered.

## Hard limits and recommendation

The frozen transcript can establish strong internal consistency, but `replicated`
requires a separately written exact GAP reconstruction. Freeze that script and its
manifest, request a bounded Lead lease, and execute only after approval. Even a
full pass can certify only a replicated one-table `PARTIAL_RESULT`; it cannot answer
revision 1 universally. Recommendation: full bounded verification under lease.
