# Triage — `L4(3)` zero-hit bounded coverage

## Claim restated

The submitted finite claim is that, in the ordinary complex character table requested from CTblLib as `L4(3)`, every one of the 29 irreducible rows and 29 conjugacy classes was checked by the exact implication `chi(C) <> 0 => |C-representative| * chi(1) divides 6065280`, yielding 495 nonzero pairs and no violation among all 841 pairs.

This is explicitly a one-table coverage claim, not a proof of the universal Kourovka assertion.

- `scope_id`: `20.115/nonzero-character-order-divisibility`
