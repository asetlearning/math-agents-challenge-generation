## Target versus witness

The target object is the class of all finite groups.  The submitted object is no
concrete witness: it is a conditional configuration in a hypothetical
least-order counterexample `G` having distinct minimal normal subgroups `M,N`.
Thus witness-equals-target is false as a closure claim.

## Subclaim decomposition

1. `P_D` is the exact fibre product `A_D x_D B_D`, and its exponent is the lcm of
   the two preimage exponents.
2. Proper `D` has a nonempty valuation-defect set, and every defect is absent at
   top height from `C`, `M`, and `N`.
3. The support equivalence is exact, and support is closed under conjugation and
   powers prime to the indexed prime.
4. Every support transversal, not merely some transversal, generates `C` with
   `D`.
5. A top-height factor carrier can be chosen to be a `p`-element; the kernels of
   `A=G/M -> C` and `B=G/N -> C` must be cross-identified correctly.
6. The three-generator theorem yields the conditional split `d(C)=3` or
   `d(C)<=2`; in the latter branch the residual obstruction is exactly the
   quantified cross-intersection of attainable loss profiles.
7. None of these subclaims proves the universal source assertion.
