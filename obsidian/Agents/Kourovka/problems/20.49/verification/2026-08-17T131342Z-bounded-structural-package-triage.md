# Verification triage — Kourovka 20.49 — bounded structural package

## Restated package

The request is to audit, at revision 1, five kinds of deliberately partial claims:

1. the frozen SmallGroups pass has complete catalogue coverage through order 255,
   14 persisted nonsoluble full-exponent pair witnesses, and zero candidates;
2. a least-order counterexample would be nonsoluble, directly indecomposable,
   exactly three-generated, and exponent-critical for proper subgroups and
   nontrivial proper quotients;
3. the order-30 dihedral group blocks the inference from the last three structural
   conditions to monolithicity;
4. a least counterexample has at least three exponent primes and, when there are
   exactly three, a prime-pure generating triple has the three distinct omitted-prime
   pair defects, while those labels are not invariant under general Nielsen moves;
5. an abelian minimal normal layer gives a one-step quotient exponent drop at its
   prime and, in a split extension, a nonzero norm/full-Jordan-block condition; the
   presented soluble exponent-900 split extension is claimed to show that this local
   condition does not force a partner for one fixed maximal 3-element.

No one-sentence claim that the universal target is closed is present. There is no
`claim-checks/<claim-id>.json` for this package and therefore no clean state-check
result. That prevents a formal universal-claim verdict but not an itemized audit of
the bounded and structural partial statements requested by Lead.

## Target versus witnesses

- **Source target:** the universal class of all finite groups, with existence of an
  at-most-two-generated subgroup of equal group exponent.
- **SG255 witness domain:** SmallGroups representatives of orders 1 through 255.
  This domain is a proper bounded subcollection of the target class, never equal to
  the universal target.
- **Persisted SG255 witnesses:** 14 concrete SmallGroups objects with coordinates in
  each object's GAP `Elements` ordering. Equality of those computed objects with the
  corresponding catalogue representatives is by construction of `SmallGroup(n,i)`,
  but the coordinates and exponent equalities still require independent checking.
- **Structural target:** a hypothetical least-order counterexample within the source
  target. These are conditional theorem claims, not computed witness objects.
- **Control witnesses:** the order-30 dihedral group, the abelian Nielsen-control
  group, and the split exponent-900 group. None is claimed equal to a counterexample;
  all are soluble controls for implications used by strategies.

Thus `witness = universal target` is false for SG255 and inapplicable for the
conditional lemmas and controls. Any final note must keep
`active_assignment_answered: no` unless a separate universal proof appears.

## Tool probe and hard limits

- GAP is present at `/usr/bin/gap`; package metadata reports GAP 4.12.1. It was not
  invoked because Lead has issued no GAP lease.
- Sage and Magma are absent.
- Python 3.12.3 is present.
- Poppler `pdftotext` and `pdftoppm` are version 24.02.0.
- `jq` is absent.

No new GAP computation is authorised. Without a lease, the 14 catalogue pair
witnesses cannot receive an independent GAP reconstruction, so the SG255 result
cannot reach `status/replicated`. A small non-heavy bespoke checker may be used only
where its finite semantics are simpler than the group claim; it cannot substitute
for SmallGrp or reimplement GAP. SmallGrp catalogue completeness is an external tool
contract quoted by the claimant and may be audited from installed documentation,
but not independently reproved here.

## Recommendation

Proceed with a full hand audit of all theorem and control-pattern claims and an
internal-completeness audit of the frozen SG255 artifacts. Issue only an itemized
partial verdict. Request a bounded GAP lease only if Lead wants independent
reconstruction of the 14 SmallGroups witnesses and a possible upgrade of that
bounded claim to `status/replicated`.
