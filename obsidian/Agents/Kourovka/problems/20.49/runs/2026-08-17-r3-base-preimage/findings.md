# Base-preimage prime-cover obstruction

## Active target

Scope: `20.49/two-generated-same-exponent`, revision 1.

Target: every finite group `G` contains a subgroup generated by at most two
elements having exponent `exp(G)`.

This note is a conditional structural partial result for a hypothetical
least-order counterexample with two distinct minimal normal subgroups. It does
not answer the active assignment.

## Reviewed starting point

Let `M,N` be distinct minimal normal subgroups and put

```text
A=G/M, B=G/N, C=G/MN.
```

The reviewed criterion identifies `G=A x_C B`, gives
`exp(G)=lcm(exp(A),exp(B))`, and characterizes a full-exponent two-generator
subgroup by arbitrary compatible factor maps over one common precomposition
orbit. Only the common base map can be required epimorphic.

## Conditional lemma

Choose an at-most-two-generated `D<=C` with `exp(D)=exp(C)`, supplied because
`C` is smaller than `G`, and write

```text
A_D=alpha^{-1}(D), B_D=beta^{-1}(D), P_D=q^{-1}(D).
```

Then

```text
P_D=A_D x_D B_D,
exp(P_D)=lcm(exp(A_D),exp(B_D)).                 (1)
```

For each prime `p`, set

```text
g_p=v_p(exp(G)), c_p=v_p(exp(C)),
s_p(D)=v_p(exp(P_D)),
S(D)={p:s_p(D)<g_p}.
```

If `D<C`, then:

1. `S(D)` is nonempty.
2. Every `p in S(D)` satisfies

   ```text
   c_p<g_p,
   v_p(exp(M))<g_p,
   v_p(exp(N))<g_p.
   ```

   Thus the missing maximal `p`-power is extension-created, not already present
   at top height in the base or either minimal kernel.
3. Define

   ```text
   Sigma_{A,p}={c in C: some lift in A has p-height g_p},
   Sigma_{B,p}={c in C: some lift in B has p-height g_p},
   Sigma_p=Sigma_{A,p} union Sigma_{B,p}.
   ```

   Then exactly

   ```text
   p in S(D) iff D cap Sigma_p is empty.         (2)
   ```

   Each support set is stable under conjugacy in `C` and under powers coprime
   to `p`.
4. For every arbitrary choice `c_p in Sigma_p`, one for each `p in S(D)`,

   ```text
   C=<D, c_p (p in S(D))>.                      (3)
   ```
5. Minimality supplies an at-most-two-generated `K_D<=P_D` with
   `exp(K_D)=exp(P_D)`. Hence the precise inverse-image defect is actually
   attained by two generators, but its common base image lies in `D<C` and is
   not epimorphic in `C`.

### Proof

Equation (1) follows from the two projection divisibilities. If `D<C`, then
`P_D<G`; if it had exponent `exp(G)`, minimality applied to `P_D` would give a
two-generator full-exponent subgroup of `G`, a contradiction. This proves (1)
and nonemptiness of `S(D)`.

The quotient `D` and the kernels `M,N` all lie in or are quotients of `P_D`, so
none can already carry `p^{g_p}` for `p in S(D)`. Formula (2) is simply the
elementwise characterization of whether `A_D` or `B_D` contains an element of
top `p`-height.

For (3), let `L` be the displayed generated subgroup. `P_D<=P_L` supplies every
top prime power outside `S(D)`, while the chosen support elements supply every
top prime power in `S(D)`. Thus `exp(P_L)=exp(G)`. Proper-subgroup exponent drop
forces `P_L=G`, hence `L=C`.

For the mixed assertion, if (say) `A` carries top `p`-height, choose a
`p`-element `x in A` of order `p^{g_p}`. Its image in `C` has order `p^t` with
`0<t<g_p`; otherwise the top power lies wholly in `N` or already in `C`.
Then `x^{p^t} in N` has order `p^{g_p-t}`.

## Complete obstruction left by this route

The known three-generator result gives `d(G)=3`, so `d(C)<=3`.

### Base-rank obstruction

If `d(C)=3`, every supplied `D` is proper and has the fixed mixed defect above.
No epimorphism `F_2->C` exists. The support-transversal identity (3) is the exact
prime-cover configuration preventing a proper base from being enlarged without
losing the two-generator bound.

### Epimorphic-profile obstruction

If `d(C)<=2`, take `D=C`. There is no group-level inverse-image defect. For an
epimorphic Nielsen orbit `O` and attainable projected exponents
`r in E_A(O), s in E_B(O)`, let

```text
L_A(r)={p:v_p(r)<g_p}, L_B(s)={p:v_p(s)<g_p}.
```

These loss sets lie in `{p:c_p<g_p}`, and the exact criterion is

```text
lcm(r,s)=exp(G) iff L_A(r) cap L_B(s)=empty.     (4)
```

Hence the residual counterexample configuration is exactly that the two
attainable loss-set families are cross-intersecting for every epimorphic orbit:

```text
L_A(r) cap L_B(s) != empty
for every O, r in E_A(O), s in E_B(O).          (5)
```

Minimality gives full-factor witnesses only over possibly non-epimorphic and
different base images. Ambient factor exponents are not thereby attainable in
an epimorphic orbit, and attainable valuation profiles have no known closure
under coordinatewise maximum. Therefore (5) cannot be replaced by intersection
or nonintersection of the two full-factor witness-orbit sets.

## What this does not establish

- It does not exclude two distinct minimal normal subgroups.
- It does not force `d(C)<=2`.
- In the `d(C)<=2` case it does not force a non-cross-intersecting pair of
  attainable profile families.
- It gives no proof or counterexample for the universal revision-1 target.
