# Bounded exclusion through order 255

## Exact partial claim

Subject to independent replication of the SmallGrp computation, there is no counterexample to Problem 20.49 of order at most 255.

This is a bounded statement only. It does not prove the universal assertion.

## Coverage argument

SmallGrp 1.5.3 documents a complete and irredundant list of isomorphism-type representatives at every order at most 2000 except 1024. The frozen script iterated every catalogue index at every order `1..255` under GAP 4.12.1.

The persistent output has exactly 255 `ORDER_START` rows, 255 `ORDER_DONE` rows, terminal order 255, no `FATAL` row, and one summary. The order rows sum to:

```text
total catalogue representatives = 7012
soluble representatives          = 6998
nonsoluble representatives       = 14
```

The source statement records the affirmative result for soluble groups. For each of the 14 nonsoluble representatives, the output contains a concrete ordered pair whose generated subgroup has exponent equal to the ambient exponent. There are 14 witness rows and zero candidate rows.

## What this does not establish

- It says nothing about order 256 or larger.
- It relies on the completeness/correctness of SmallGrp and GAP's group, solvability, and exponent routines.
- The 14 witness coordinates use GAP's `Elements(G)` ordering and should be reconstructed with the recorded GAP/SmallGrp versions or replaced by explicit permutations in independent review.
- The 6,998 soluble rows use the affirmative soluble theorem quoted in the source; the computation did not construct a witness for each of them.
- It is not independently replicated and remains `status/conjectured`.
