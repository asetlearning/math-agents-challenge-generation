## Hand reconstruction before the leased computation

Let `V=F_3^3`. The standard order formula gives

`|SL(3,3)|=((3^3-1)(3^3-3)(3^3-3^2))/(3-1)=5616`.

Its centre has order `gcd(3,3-1)=1`, so `SL(3,3)=PSL(3,3)`. The standard simplicity theorem for `PSL_n(q)` (excluding only `(n,q)=(2,2),(2,3)`) makes this group finite nonabelian simple.

Every involution in characteristic three is semisimple. Determinant one and nonidentity force eigenvalue multiplicities `(+1)^1,(-1)^2`. Thus it is determined by a direct-sum decomposition `V=P direct_sum H`, where `P` is a projective point and `H` a projective line not containing `P`. Conversely every such anti-flag gives an involution. There are `13` points and, for each, `9` nonincident lines, hence `|D|=117`. `GL(3,3)` is transitive on these decompositions. Its involution centralizer is `GL(1,3) x GL(2,3)` and has surjective determinant, so the `GL` class does not split on intersection with `SL`; therefore this is the unique `L`-class of involutions. This also predicts `|C_L(t)|=96/2=48` and class size `5616/48=117`.

For an anti-flag choose nonzero `u` spanning `P` and a form `f` with kernel `H`, normalized by `f(u)=1`. Its involution is `t(u,f)=2*u tensor f-I`. For a second normalized anti-flag `(v,g)`, put `alpha=f(v)`, `beta=g(u)`, and `k=alpha*beta`. The product fixes `ker(f) intersect ker(g)`, and its remaining quadratic factor is controlled by `k`. Direct expansion predicts the complete off-diagonal colour rule:

- `alpha=beta=0`: order `2`;
- exactly one of `alpha,beta` is zero: order `6` (a nontrivial characteristic-three Jordan part at eigenvalue `-1`);
- `alpha*beta=1`: order `3`;
- `alpha*beta=-1`: order `4`.

Counting second anti-flags from a fixed vertex predicts valencies `12,32,24,48` for orders `2,3,4,6`, respectively, and hence global edge counts `702,1872,1404,2808`. These are pre-computation predictions, not reported output. The matrix computation must agree or the run stops for diagnosis.

The standard automorphism-side prediction is order `11232`: `PGL(3,3)=PSL(3,3)` supplies the inner action of order `5616`, the field automorphism group is trivial, and inverse-transpose supplies the graph automorphism of order two. The exact checker does not assume that this equals the colour group.
