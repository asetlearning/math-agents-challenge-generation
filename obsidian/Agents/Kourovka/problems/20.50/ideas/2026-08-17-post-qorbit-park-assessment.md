# Obstruction

The obstruction is a missing global closure or termination invariant. The linked
partials make every fixed derived truncation finite, but the controlling bound
depends on the derived level. QORBIT-CLOSURE tried to replace that tower by a
finite rank-three orbit and is semantically refuted: conjugation by the fourth
involution can create a nonzero four-support correction. A useful 19-minute test
must therefore control those corrections globally; checking another quotient,
another fixed layer, or another finite-radius orbit is only a necessary proxy.

# Candidate portfolio

## 1. DEFECT-FIBRE — refine closure by allowing a finite correction fibre

**Mode:** theoretical refinement of QORBIT and the normal-closure route.

**Idea.** Put (H=\langle a,b,c\rangle), (S=d^H), and

\[
K_0=\langle [s,t]:s,t\in S\rangle.
\]

Instead of the false equality (S^d=S), try to show that (K_0) is abelian and
normalized by (d). It is already normalized by (H). Also
(s^d=[d,s]s\in K_0S), so these two properties would make (K_0S) stable under
the marked generators.

**Why it might work.** From general mathematical knowledge (unverified): if the
two properties hold, then (K_0) is a finite elementary abelian group because it
has finitely many involutory generators; the normal closure of (d) modulo
(K_0) is a finite elementary abelian group generated by (S). Since (H) is
finite by the linked rank-three bound, this would give a finite conjugacy/normal
closure and hence a target-facing finiteness reduction. The nonzero QORBIT defect
is allowed: it becomes a fibre element rather than a contradiction.

**Cheap falsifier.** Derive one generic formula for
([s,t]^d=[s^d,t^d]). Kill the idea if it introduces a commutator outside
(K_0) with no strictly decreasing rewrite, or if an admissible hand image makes
([K_0,K_0]\ne1) or (K_0^d\nleq K_0).

**Cost.** At least 45--90 hand-reasoning minutes; no heavy slot. Twelve research
minutes plus seven packaging minutes do not cover the two universal identities.

**Self-critique.** The two desired properties amount to terminating the same
normal-closure derived tower in a different notation. The linked descent supplies
no new relation forcing them, and a one-generator calculation would not quantify
over all (s,t\in S). This is not a cost-effective refinement now.

## 2. INN-4T — representation-changing pivot to inner permutation geometry

**Mode:** structured representation change.

**Idea.** Replace elements of (G_4) by their conjugation permutations on the full
set of involutions. Let (D=\{\iota_x:x^2=1\}\subseteq\operatorname{Inn}(G_4)).
The target-facing observable is the closure size of the four marked points under
the four marked permutations, together with a completeness certificate that a
finite orbit ball is closed.

**Why it might work.** From general mathematical knowledge (unverified): the
elements of (D) are involutions and every product of two has order dividing four,
so this is a rank-four 4-transposition permutation geometry. Finite closure would
make (G_4/Z(G_4)) finite; the centralizer/Schur mechanism already recorded in the
linked QORBIT note would then reduce the target to a finite group.

**Cheap falsifier.** Kill a proposed finite closure rule upon a hand-derived new
point outside its claimed closed ball, or upon a marked admissible action with an
unbounded orbit. Mere failure to close at a fixed radius is not a falsifier of the
whole representation.

**Cost.** Multi-hour hand classification or a separately authorized finite
enumeration/certificate search. The present request forbids computation, and the
linked records provide no finite state space or classification theorem.

**Self-critique.** Local pair orders need not bound a global four-generator
geometry. The linked QORBIT image already shows that a natural rank-three slice is
not invariant. In 19 minutes, a finite-radius drawing would change representation
but not information: without a completeness argument it is only another necessary
proxy.

## 3. AFFINE-INFINITE — switch to the counterexample direction

**Mode:** structured infinitude construction.

**Idea.** Seek an infinite generalized-dihedral or affine group generated by four
reflections, because products of reflections become translations and the
all-involutions law can be checked structurally rather than pair by pair.

**Why it might work.** From general mathematical knowledge (unverified): in
(A\rtimes\langle r\rangle), with (A) abelian and (r) acting by inversion,
the product of two reflections is their translation difference. This turns the
fourth-power condition into an exponent-four condition on the translation part.

**Cheap falsifier.** The cheapest family dies immediately: four involutions in a
generalized-dihedral group with (A) of exponent at most four generate a
translation subgroup generated by finitely many differences and 2-torsion marks,
hence a finite subgroup. An infinite witness would need a genuinely nonabelian
module/semidirect mechanism and a complete classification of all its involutions.

**Cost.** The family kill is under ten minutes but is not a target advance. A
broader construction with an all-involutions certificate does not fit 19 minutes.

**Self-critique.** Excluding one natural affine family says little about the
universal group. Relaxing the exponent-four translation condition would usually
break the semantic law, so there is no honest bounded next construction in the
linked context.
