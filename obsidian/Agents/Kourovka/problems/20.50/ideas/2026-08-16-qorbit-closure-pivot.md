# Exactly one pivot: QORBIT-CLOSURE

## Direction

**Finite conjugation-quandle orbit closure.** Replace the derived-series filtration
by the conjugation action on involutions.

For a generic marked split

\[
G=\langle a,b,c,d\rangle,\qquad H=\langle a,b,c\rangle,
\qquad \mathcal O=d^H,
\]

the reviewed rank-three bound gives \(|H|\le 2^{11}\), hence
\(|\mathcal O|\le2^{11}\). The set \(\mathcal O\) is already \(H\)-invariant. The
experiment has one exact observable:

\[
\mathfrak D=(\mathcal O)^d\setminus\mathcal O.
\tag{Q}
\]

It asks whether the conditional involution law forces \(\mathfrak D=\varnothing\)
by a terminating conjugation-quandle rewrite, uniformly in every admissible marked
group. Equivalently, construct, for every \(h\in H\), a word
\(\tau(h)\in H\) with

\[
(d^h)^d=d^{\tau(h)},
\tag{Q1}
\]

and derive that the construction is independent of the chosen word for \(h\).

## Why a pass advances the exact universal target

From general mathematical knowledge (unverified here): if \((Q)\) holds, then
\(\mathcal O\) is invariant under \(H\) and \(d\), hence is the full conjugacy orbit
of \(d\). Applying the label-symmetric argument to all four marked generators gives

\[
[G_4:C_{G_4}(x_i)]\le2^{11}\quad(1\le i\le4),
\]

so the intersection of those four centralizers, which is \(Z(G_4)\), has index at
most \(2^{44}\). Schur's theorem then makes \(G_4'\) finite; and
\(G_4/G_4'\) is generated by four involutions, so it has order at most \(2^4\).
Thus a pass would imply that \(G_4\) is finite and supply a universal upper-side reduction,
not another finite quotient. Exact order would still require a subsequent finite
certificate, but the present missing finiteness gate would be crossed.

The quandle rules available without computation are
\(u\triangleright v=vuv\), involutivity and self-distributivity of right
translations, and the four-braid consequence
\(uvuv=vuvu\) for every pair of involutions. This is a different representation
from the nonuniform module/derived descent.

# The 63-minute experiment

- **0--9 minutes:** write the reduction `(Q) => G_4 finite` in full, including the
  four centralizer indices, their intersection, and the exact point where Schur's
  theorem is invoked.
- **9--19 minutes:** encode the involution law as quandle/right-translation rules
  and define a well-founded measure that first counts occurrences of \(d\) in a
  conjugating word and then word length. Equal-length braid cycling is not progress.
- **19--44 minutes:** seek a uniform induction deriving \((Q1)\): base \(h=1\), then a
  single extension rule from \(h\) to \(he\) for
  \(e\in\{a,b,c\}\). Every rewrite used in the extension must strictly decrease the
  declared measure, and all three generator choices must be covered.
- **44--54 minutes:** attack the proposed extension rule at the first full-support
  critical pairs (all of \(a,b,c,d\) present). Reject any argument that silently
  makes the normal closure abelian or \(G_4\) metabelian, since the reviewed
  nontrivial second-derived quotient data rules out that shortcut.
- **54--63 minutes:** write exactly one certificate below and stop.

## Success certificate

`QORBIT_CLOSE`: a finite list of quandle rewrite identities, a well-founded
termination measure, the base case and the generator-extension rule deriving \((Q1)\),
and the complete centralizer/Schur deduction of finiteness. The proof must quantify
over an arbitrary admissible marked group; a check in \(D\), \(E\), or any other
finite quotient is not a success certificate.

## Failure certificate

`QORBIT_FAIL`: either

1. an explicit \(h\in H\) and a hand-only admissible quotient/invariant showing
   \((d^h)^d\notin d^H\); or
2. a nonjoinable full-support critical pair for the proposed decreasing rewrite,
   with both branches and the separating invariant written out.

Quotient evidence may only falsify the rewrite; producing or multiplying further
finite quotients is not the experiment.

## Hard kill

At active minute 44, abandon any proof attempt that lacks a strictly decreasing
generator-extension rule. At minute 54, if neither `QORBIT_CLOSE` nor a hand
`QORBIT_FAIL` is available, record the shortest unresolved full-support term as
`QORBIT_NO_CERT`, classify this named strategy `STRATEGY_EXHAUSTED`, spend only the
last nine minutes documenting it, and stop at cumulative 180/180. No GAP, web,
history, delegate, extra quotient product, or extension request is part of this
experiment.

is still worth one bounded attempt because a uniform orbit-closure lemma would
