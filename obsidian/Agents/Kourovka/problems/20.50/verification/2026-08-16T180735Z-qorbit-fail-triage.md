## Target versus witness

The source target is the universal four-involution group \(G_4\). The witness is the source-defined \(U_3\) equipped with a redundant fourth mark. They are not claimed or proved equal:

`witness_equals_target: false`.

The only potentially valid inference is contravariant at the level of identities: a uniform identity in \(G_4\) must survive every admissible marked quotient. That descent is a separate sub-claim to verify.

## Sub-claim decomposition

1. The canonical \(U_3\) independently satisfies the law for every pair of involutions.
2. The marks \(e,f,[e,f],d\) are exact, pairwise distinct involutions and generate \(U_3\).
3. The already-reviewed facts \(\gamma_5(U_3)=1\) and \(U_3''\cong C_2^3\) force \(z=[[d,e],[d,f]]\ne1\).
4. With the stated commutator and conjugation conventions, polarization gives the exact signed identity
   \((d^{ef})^d=d^{ef}z\).
5. In \(U_3/U_3''=H_3\), the cube-cycle model gives a trivial stabilizer of \(\bar d\) in \(\langle\bar e,\bar f\rangle\), so no alternate orbit representative absorbs \(z\).
6. A uniform QORBIT-CLOSURE identity in the universal four-marked group descends to this independently admissible marked image.
7. Even if 1–6 pass, the exact order and finiteness of \(G_4\) remain open.
