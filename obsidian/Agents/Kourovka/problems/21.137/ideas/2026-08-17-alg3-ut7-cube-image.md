# ALG3-UT7-CUBE-IMAGE

## Decision first

I recommend the **counterexample direction** for the first 60-minute increment, with exactly one experiment: test a bounded family of two-generator nilpotent associative algebras inside `UT_7(F_3)` and use the algebra-group cube image itself as the exact observable.

My judgement is that this has higher one-hour expected value than another proof attempt.  The authorized minimum-counterexample audit leaves closure automatic in the cyclic-center branch and off the radical of `beta`, while the radical fibres still have no forcing identity.  The authorized positive results stop at class `5` for `p=3`, and the frozen class-`6` Hall lift met its exact kill.  The algebra-group model changes representation, lands precisely at the first uncovered class-`6` boundary, and can return a target-equal finite witness rather than another necessary condition.

## The obstruction being attacked

The hard gate is not generation by cubes but closure of the **actual cube-value set**.  A catalogue or presentation search can silently replace that set by the verbal subgroup.  In an algebra group `1+J`, the actual cube set has an exact finite description, so closure and noncommutativity can be tested without that substitution.

## The single experiment

Work over `F_3`.  Let `E_ij` be the standard `7 x 7` matrix units and set

`S = E_12+E_23+E_34+E_45+E_56+E_67`,

`T_tau = tau_1 E_12+tau_2 E_23+tau_3 E_34+tau_4 E_45+tau_5 E_56+tau_6 E_67`

for `tau=(tau_1,...,tau_6) in F_3^6`.  Let `J_tau` be the nonunital associative `F_3`-subalgebra of strictly upper-triangular matrices generated by `S,T_tau`, and let `G_tau=1+J_tau`.

The bounded family for this increment is exactly

`F_12={tau in F_3^6 : tau_1 tau_2 tau_3 != tau_4 tau_5 tau_6 and dim(J_tau)<=12}`.

There are only `3^6=729` parameter rows before the two exact filters.  Do not raise the dimension bound or add a third generator during this increment.

For each retained `tau`, compute exactly

`X_tau={x^3:x in J_tau}`

and the circle subgroup

`K_tau=<X_tau>_circle`, where `u circle v=u+v+uv`.

The primary observable is the integer

`Delta_tau=|K_tau|-|X_tau|`.

Because `X_tau` is contained in its generated circle subgroup, `Delta_tau=0` is equivalent to `1+X_tau` itself being a subgroup of `G_tau`.  This is the closure test for the actual cube values, not for their generated subgroup.

Use the exact leading-layer prefilter before full enumeration.  With `V_tau=span_F3{S,T_tau}`, let

`C_tau={v^3:v in V_tau}` in the third superdiagonal.

If `C_tau` is not additively closed, reject `tau` and record `v,w in V_tau` with `v^3+w^3 notin C_tau`.  Indeed, the degree-`3` component of a cube depends only on the degree-`1` component, while the product of two cube values starts in degree `6`; circle-closure of `X_tau` would therefore force additive closure of `C_tau`.

The nonabelian observable is already built into the parameter filter:

`S^3 T_tau^3-T_tau^3 S^3`

has `E_17` coefficient

`tau_4 tau_5 tau_6-tau_1 tau_2 tau_3`.

Thus the two displayed actual cube values do not commute whenever `tau` is retained.
