# Constraint-sensitive assessment

The bound depends on the full active hypotheses, not merely on the generated power subgroup.

1. The prime satisfies \(p>2\). This is required upstream for the class-two exponent-\(p\) Lie/symplectic model and excludes the separate square-set problem.
2. The quotient-minimal group \(H\) still has exponent exactly \(p^2\): it inherits exponent dividing \(p^2\), while its nonabelian power image is nontrivial, so some element has nontrivial \(p\)-th power.
3. The actual value set is a subgroup \(Q\), hence \(Q=H^p\), \(\exp Q=p\), and every element of \(Q\) has an actual \(p\)-th root in \(H\). Replacing this by the verbal subgroup generated by powers would destroy the covering step.

In the quotient-minimal model, \(Q'=C\cong C_p\) is central and \(Q\) has class two. Thus

\[
U=Q/Z(Q)
\]

is a nonzero symplectic \(\mathbf F_p\)-space of dimension \(2d\). For every order-\(p\) cyclic subgroup \(K\le R=H/Q\), the cyclic-coset lemma places all projected power values with root coset in \(K\) inside a totally isotropic subspace \(W_K\), so \(|W_K|\le p^d\).

Actual root-surjectivity makes the \(W_K\) cover every nonzero vector of \(U\): a nonzero value cannot have a root in the identity coset because elements of \(Q\) have \(p\)-th power one. Counting nonzero vectors gives at least \(p^d+1\) cyclic subgroups. Since \(R\) has exponent \(p\), even if it is nonabelian its nonidentity elements partition into order-\(p\) cyclic subgroups. Therefore

\[
\frac{|R|-1}{p-1}\ge p^d+1,
\qquad |R|>p^d.
\]

As \(|R|\) is a power of \(p\),

\[
\boxed{[H:Q]\ge p^{d+1}}.
\]

I found no additional gap in this counting argument. It is a structural index bound, generally weaker than the accepted affine-rank bound, and it does not imply \(d=0\) or \(Q\) abelian.

# Sharp limitation

The symplectic-spread construction shows that \(p^d+1\) maximal isotropic subspaces can cover a nonzero \(2d\)-dimensional symplectic space exactly. Thus no improvement follows from isotropy and cardinality alone. Any stronger argument must use compatibility of the basepoints, root actions, and the central extension, not just the cover.

The result respects all seven canonical constraints but establishes only a necessary condition for a hypothetical counterexample. The active conclusion `21.137-odd-P-abelian` remains open, and novelty is uncertain under discovery blindness.
