# Triage — Kourovka 21.137 same-coset commutativity

## Claim restated

In the checked quotient-minimal model, for every coset (xQ\subseteq H), any two actual (p)-th powers whose roots lie in that coset commute in (Q).

## Method and limit

The claim combines the affine power formula modulo (C=Q'), the class-2 exponent-(p) Lie algebra of (Q), the symplectic commutator form on (Q/Z(Q)), and adjoint linear algebra for (N=A-I). A pass yields a new structural restriction on each root coset, but does not itself improve the accepted affine covering count or establish that all of (Q) is abelian.
