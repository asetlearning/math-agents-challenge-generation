## Partial A — regular wreath products cannot supply the witness

Let (H) be any nonabelian group of exponent 3 and
(W=H\wr C_3=H^3\rtimes\langle t\rangle), where (t) cyclically permutes the
coordinates. Direct collection gives

\[
\{w^3:w\in W\}=\{1\}\cup
\{(h,h^r,h^s):h,r,s\in H\}.
\]

For the forward containment, the coordinates of (((h_1,h_2,h_3)t)^3) are cyclic
permutations of (h_1h_2h_3), hence conjugate. Conversely, after fixing the first
product (h), the two partial products allow the two conjugators to be selected
independently. The (t^2)-coset gives the same set, and base elements cube to 1.

Choose (h,r\in H) with (h^r\ne h). Both
((h,h^r,h)) and ((h^{-1},h^{-1},h^{-1})) are actual cubes, while their product

\[
(1,h^rh^{-1},1)
\]

is not: all three coordinates of any nonidentity displayed cube are conjugate, and
only 1 is conjugate to 1. Thus the actual cube set is not a subgroup.

For (H=H_3(3)), the resulting group has order (3^{10}) and exact exponent 9,
so it is a particularly close `OUT_OF_SCOPE_EXAMPLE` above the reviewed order
threshold.

## Partial B — a filtered algebra group has noncommuting cubes but not closure

Over (mathbf F_3), put

\[
x=e_{12}+e_{23}+e_{34},\qquad y=e_{45}+e_{56}+e_{67},
\]

and let (J\) be the associative subalgebra of strictly upper-triangular
(7\times7) matrices generated by (x,y). Its 15-element basis consists of the
nonconstant monomials (x^iy^j), (0\le i,j\le3), with
(x^4=y^4=yx=0). Hence (G_J=1+J) has order (3^{15}), exponent dividing 9
because (J^7=0), and exact exponent 9 because (x^3\ne0).

Every actual cube is (1+a^3). The cubes (1+x^3) and (1+y^3) do not commute:
(x^3y^3=e_{17}\ne0), whereas (y^3x^3=0). Their product is nevertheless not a
cube. In the word-degree filtration, writing
(a=\alpha x+\beta y+a_{\ge2}), the degree-3 part of (a^3) is

\[
\alpha x^3+\alpha^2\beta x^2y+
\alpha\beta^2xy^2+\beta y^3.
\]

The degree-3 part of (x^3+y^3+x^3y^3) forces
(alpha=\beta=1), which also forces both mixed coefficients to be 1. This
contradicts the absence of mixed degree-3 terms. Therefore the product of these two
actual cubes is not an actual cube.

## What is covered and what is not

Covered, pending Validator audit:

- every regular wreath product (H\wr C_3) with nonabelian exponent-3 base (H)
  is excluded as an active-scope witness;
- the explicit 15-dimensional two-chain algebra group (1+J) is excluded, despite
  containing a noncommuting pair of actual cubes;
- the minimal direct split-holomorph realization logged in the run has only
  abelian cubes.

Not covered:

- nonsplit pc extensions at the least order (3^9);
- other primes or other class-at-least-4 constructions;
- a proof that closure always forces commutativity;
- any active-scope counterexample.
