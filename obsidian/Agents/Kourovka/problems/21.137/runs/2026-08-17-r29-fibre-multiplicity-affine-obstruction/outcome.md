# Exact certificate

For any elementary normal section `V=N/K`, a normalized section of
`G/K -> G/N` gives, in the `q`-root fibre,

```text
(v sigma(q))^p=(lambda_q+N_q v)sigma(q^p),
N_q=1+M_q+...+M_q^(p-1),
m_q(w)=|K||ker N_q| 1_[w in lambda_q+im N_q].
```

Under `sigma'(q)=u_q sigma(q)`, with `t=q^p`,

```text
lambda'_q=lambda_q+N_q u_q-u_t,
m'_q(w')=m_q(w'+u_t).
```

Thus root-origin changes preserve the weighted support and target-origin
changes translate it; no unproved coordinate invariance is used.

For `mu_t=sum_[q^p=t]m_q`, the required exact double count is

```text
sum_[w in a+W] mu_t(w)
 = |K| sum_[q^p=t] |ker N_q|
       |(a+W) intersect (lambda_q+im N_q)|.
```

Its character form is

```text
hat(mu_t)(chi)
 = |K||V| sum_[q^p=t, chi|im(N_q)=1] chi(lambda_q).
```

If the literal power image is a subgroup, its support in a fixed target fibre
is an affine coset of its intersection with `V`.  This gives positivity on
that coset and zero off it, but supplies no equality among the positive
weights and no vanishing of the displayed character sums.

# Explicit method-failure model

For every odd `p`, take `V=F_p^2` with the nondegenerate alternating form
`beta((x,y),(x',y'))=xy'-x'y`, and use the exponent-`p` Heisenberg label group

```text
H=V x F_p,
(v,z)(v',z')=(v+v',z+z'+(1/2)beta(v,v')).
```

Index formal quotient-root fibres by `(a,b,c,d,e) in F_p^5`.  On the selected
section take `M_q=I`, hence `N_q=0`, take a deeper factor `|K|=p`, and give the
fibre the full label

```text
((a^(p-1)b,c^(p-1)d),e) in H.
```

Each fibre has exact weight `p^3`.  Writing `h(0)=2p-1` and
`h(x)=p-1` for `x!=0`, the resulting full weight is

```text
r((x,y),z)=p^3 h(x)h(y).
```

It has support exactly the nonabelian subgroup `H`, is positive and
nonconstant, has nonisotropic commutator image, satisfies the total double
count `sum_H r=p^8`, and respects inversion and Heisenberg conjugacy.  Its
Fourier transform is nonzero at every character, so it is not constant along
any nonzero translation subspace.  It satisfies every affine, covariance,
support, weighting, and symmetry row derived above.

This is an abstract incidence model, not an asserted factor system, group, or
counterexample.  Its role is exact: it shows that closure plus actual affine
root multiplicities does not force the desired weight constancy.  A proof
would need additional cross-`q` multiplication identities not furnished by
the named observable.
