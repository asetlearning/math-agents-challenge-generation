# Active target

Scope: `21.137/odd-prime-exponent-p2`, revision 2. At \(p=3\), seek a
finite group of exact exponent nine whose literal cube-value set is a
nonabelian subgroup.

# Partial result

Let

\[
 P=H_3(3)\times C_3^3.
\]

There is no extension

\[
 1\longrightarrow P\longrightarrow G\longrightarrow A\longrightarrow1
\]

with \(A\) elementary abelian for which the literal cube-value set of \(G\)
is \(P\). In fact, in every such extension of exponent dividing nine, all
actual cubes commute, whether or not their set is closed.

This excludes, in particular, the completely frozen next carrier
\(A=C_3^5\), \(|G|=3^{11}\). It does not answer the unrestricted source
scope.

# The complete extension model

The full action, factor-set, associativity, every-coset cube formula, and
literal union condition are recorded in `scratch/carrier-equations.md`.
In brief, a normalized section gives

\[
 (x,u)(y,v)=\bigl(x\alpha_u(y)f(u,v),u+v\bigr),
\]

with

\[
 \alpha_u\alpha_v=c_{f(u,v)}\alpha_{u+v},
\qquad
 f(u,v)f(u+v,w)=\alpha_u(f(v,w))f(u,v+w),
\]

and the exact cube map

\[
 C_u(x)=x\alpha_u(x)f(u,u)\alpha_{2u}(x)f(2u,u).
\]

Thus the literal cube set is

\[
 S=\{1\}\cup\bigcup_{u\ne0}C_u(P),
\]

not any one norm image and not the subgroup generated by those images.

# Proof of the family exclusion

Write \(k=\mathbb F_3\),

\[
 P=V\oplus Z,
 \quad V=k^2,
 \quad Z=kc\oplus k^3,
\]

with multiplication

\[
 (v,z)(v',z')=(v+v',z+z'+2\det(v,v')c).
\]

Then

\[
 [(v,z),(v',z')]=(0,\det(v,v')c).                               \tag{1}
\]

For \(u\in A\), let \(M_u\) be the action induced by \(\alpha_u\) on
\(V=P/Z(P)\). Inner automorphisms act trivially on \(V\), so the action
equations make

\[
 M:A\longrightarrow GL_2(3)                                    \tag{2}
\]

a homomorphism. Also \(M_u^3=I\).

## 1. The nontrivial induced-action case

Project the complete cube formula to \(V\). Since
\(M_{2u}=M_u^2\), it is

\[
 \overline{C_u(x)}
 =\ell(u)+(I+M_u+M_u^2)\bar x.                                  \tag{3}
\]

In dimension two, \(M_u^3=I\) in characteristic three implies
\((M_u-I)^2=0\). Hence

\[
 I+M_u+M_u^2=(M_u-I)^2=0.                                      \tag{4}
\]

So every cube rooted in the same coset \(u\) has one common noncentral
label \(\ell(u)\in V\).

For \(a\in A\), conjugation by a lift of \(a\) preserves every root coset,
because \(A\) is abelian. It sends the cube label \(\ell(u)\) to
\(M_a\ell(u)\). By (3)--(4) that new root in the same coset still has label
\(\ell(u)\). Therefore

\[
 \ell(u)\in V^{M(A)}\qquad(u\in A).                             \tag{5}
\]

Now \(|GL_2(3)|=48\), so a nontrivial 3-subgroup has order three and its
common fixed space on \(V\) is one-dimensional. If \(M(A)\ne1\), (5) puts
all cube labels on one line. Equation (1) then shows that every two actual
cubes commute.

## 2. The trivial induced-action case

Assume \(M(A)=1\). Fix \(u\) with nonzero cube label \(q=\ell(u)\).
Relative to \(V\oplus Z\), write

\[
 \alpha_u(v,z)=(v,(I+R)z+Lv).
\]

Every such automorphism fixes \(c\). Since a section lift commutes with its
cube,

\[
 \alpha_u^3=c_{s(u)^3}.
\]

On blocks this is exactly

\[
 R^3=0,
 \qquad
 R^2L=J_q,
 \qquad
 J_q(v)=\det(q,v)c.                                              \tag{6}
\]

For \(q\ne0\), (6) makes \(R^2\ne0\). On the four-dimensional space
\(Z\), \(R\) therefore has Jordan type \(J_3+J_1\). Choose a basis
\(e_1,e_2,c,d\) such that

\[
 Re_1=e_2,
 \quad Re_2=c,
 \quad Rc=Rd=0.                                                  \tag{7}
\]

Now take another label \(q'=\ell(v)\ne0\), and write its action as
\((I+R',L')\). The outer action of the abelian quotient says that these two
automorphisms commute modulo an inner automorphism. Hence

\[
 RR'=R'R,
 \qquad
 RL'-R'L=J_h                                                     \tag{8}
\]

for some \(h\in V\). In particular the second map in (8) is zero modulo
\(kc\).

Every nilpotent \(R'\) commuting with (7) and fixing \(c\) has the form

\[
\begin{aligned}
 R'e_1&=b e_2+\gamma c+\delta d,\\
 R'e_2&=b c,\\
 R'c&=0,\\
 R'd&=\varepsilon c.
\end{aligned}                                                    \tag{9}
\]

Thus

\[
 R'^2e_1=(b^2+\delta\varepsilon)c,                               \tag{10}
\]

and \(R'^2\) kills \(e_2,c,d\).

Let \(x(t)\) and \(x'(t)\) be the \(e_1\)-coefficients of \(L(t)\)
and \(L'(t)\). Equations (6) and its primed version say

\[
 x(t)=\det(q,t),
 \qquad
 (b^2+\delta\varepsilon)x'(t)=\det(q',t).                        \tag{11}
\]

Reducing the second equation in (8) modulo \(kc\), and using (7)--(9), gives

\[
 x'(t)=b x(t),
 \qquad
 \delta x(t)=0\quad(t\in V).                                   \tag{12}
\]

Because \(q\ne0\), the functional \(x\) is nonzero, so \(\delta=0\).
Because \(q'\ne0\), (10)--(11) then force \(b\ne0\). Combining
(11)--(12), and using \(b^3=b\) in \(\mathbb F_3\), gives

\[
 \det(q',t)=b\det(q,t)\quad(t\in V),
\]

hence

\[
 q'=bq.                                                          \tag{13}
\]

Thus every pair of nonzero cube labels is linearly dependent. All labels lie
on one line in \(V\), and (1) again says that all actual cubes commute.

The two cases exhaust (2), proving the family exclusion.

# Exact frontier

For the reviewed quotient-minimal layer with
\(P\cong H_3(3)\times C_3^3\), an elementary abelian root quotient is now
excluded at every rank, including the first bounded carrier \(C_3^5\).
A model at this kernel layer would therefore need a **nonabelian** exponent-
three quotient \(G/P\). Alternatively, the kernel must move to a larger or
different class-two exponent-three type. No such remaining family is claimed
excluded here.

# What this does not establish

- It does not prove the odd-prime scope.
- It does not exclude nonabelian exponent-three quotients \(G/P\).
- It does not exclude a larger or different power subgroup \(P\).
- It does not turn the earlier local action covers into groups; the proof starts
  from the complete associative extension equations.
- No computation, catalogue, web search, or solution-bearing history was used.

# How the partial result could be wrong

1. A sign/order error in passing from the nonabelian factor law to the action
   equation could affect (8), although its reduction modulo the central line is
   insensitive to the inner factor's sign.
2. The centralizer normal form (9) must include every nilpotent endomorphism
   commuting with a \(J_3+J_1\) block; the derivation uses both \(R'c=0\) and
   \(R'^3=0\).
3. Constancy in (3) uses the quotient being elementary abelian and the
   noncentral quotient having dimension exactly two.
4. The family exclusion must not be promoted to the unrestricted source target.
