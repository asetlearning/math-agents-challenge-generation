# Active statement

Construct or rule out a finite group (G) with normal, abstractly isomorphic
subgroups (K,L), both of index 12, such that (G/K\cong C_{12}) and
(G/L\cong A_4).

# Direction and first strategy

The planning estimate is 0.55 that such a triple exists, so this run starts in the
constructive proof direction. Work from two compatible epimorphisms and their
fibre-product/Goursat or extension data. Do not begin by extending an undirected
SmallGroups catalogue range. A useful first result is an exact compatibility
reduction with a finite parameter space and a certificate plan.
