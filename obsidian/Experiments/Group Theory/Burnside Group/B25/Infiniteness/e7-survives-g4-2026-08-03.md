## Result

`E7 = [a,b,b,b,b,b,b]` (weight-7 Engel word) is **nontrivial in**
`G4 = G3 / <<p_i^5>>`, where the five length-4 periods are
`p_1=AABB, p_2=AAbb, p_3=ABAb, p_4=ABaB, p_5=ABab`.

Equivalently: E7 is not a product of conjugates of fifth powers of words of length <= 4, with
**arbitrary** conjugators and **arbitrarily many** factors.

Validator status: `#status/proven` — [[2026-08-03-b25-gate2-FINAL-RULING-E7-survives-G4]]
(final-read PASS). First **certified** per-level period-band small-cancellation step at exponent 5.

## Why this problem (the reduction chain)

1. **Kernel.** R(2,5) has order 5^34 and nilpotency **class 12** (folklore "13" corrected here,
   vs Havas–Wall–Wamsley 1974 + independent GAP). Hence `ker(B(2,5) -> R(2,5)) = gamma_13`, so
   **B(2,5) infinite <=> gamma_13 != 1**.
2. **Witness.** Kourovka 11.48 (Kostrikin, still open) asks whether E7 is a product of fifth
   powers. In R(2,5): `E7 = 1` but `[x,5y] != 1` — the Engel boundary is exactly 6. So **if
   E7 != 1 in B(2,5), then B(2,5) is infinite**.
3. **Ladder.** `E7 != 1 in B(2,5)  <=>  E7 != 1 in G_L for EVERY L`, where
   `G_L = F2/<<w^5 : |w| <= L>>`. Each rung is decidable-in-principle; the limit is the open
   problem. → [[B25-E7-ladder-mirror]]

## Step-by-step process (how the G4 proof works)

Proof by contradiction over van Kampen diagrams. Assume `E7 = 1` in G4; take a **minimal** relative
diagram D over `G3 + {p_i^5}` with boundary the G3 normal form of E7 (length 128). Then:

1. **Small pieces.** Distinct-period overlaps have length <= 3 (below the C'(1/6) threshold).
   All difficulty is **same-period** overlaps — exactly why classical small cancellation fails
   on Burnside presentations.
2. **Orientation (SO).** Every long same-period contact is opposite-oriented (no period is
   cyclically conjugate to its inverse — checked exhaustively, zero collisions).
3. **Annuli.** `E(p_i) = <p_i>` to conjugator length 24 (only period-power connectors, zero
   inverting) ⇒ same-period annuli are dipoles, excluded.
4. **Phase absorption (H2).** Every phase connector is either a single-cell cap of length <= 3
   (cap-conjugator census: only the trivial rotation conjugator, zero exotic) OR a multi-cell
   "beaded" bridge of <= 3-letter ordinary pieces (harmless per-piece). H2 turns every
   phase-offset pocket into an aligned pocket.
5. **Aligned bands vanish (crux).** An adjacent opposite same-period pair is phase-aligned, so
   its outer contour freely reduces to the empty word ⇒ a van Kampen **cancellable pair**;
   removing it keeps the same boundary with two fewer cells, contradicting minimality. Verified
   **general** (all contact lengths 4..19, all five periods) — see receipt below.
6. **Internal contraction (BR2).** Harmless internal disc bands compress; residual pieces <= 3.
7. **Ordinary C'(1/6) disc.** After 4–6, every realized piece has length <= 3; since relators
   have perimeter 20 and 3/20 < 1/6, the remaining disc is C'(1/6).
8. **Greendlinger.** (Lyndon–Schupp, Ch. V, Thm 4.5.) Some boundary cell exposes >= 12
   consecutive relator letters — a strict Dehn arc.
9. **Transfer.** Reductions preserve the boundary, so the exposure transfers to a strict Dehn
   arc on E7's normal form.
10. **Contradiction.** E7's normal form is geodesic and was machine-scanned against all 400
    strict-arc patterns: **zero hits** (longest overlap 7/20). No such cell exists ⇒ no diagram
    exists ⇒ **E7 != 1 in G4.** ∎

## What was achieved

- A genuine new theorem: **E7 != 1 in G4**, the first **certified** small-cancellation step at
  exponent 5 — a regime where published theorems provably do not apply (Agol–Groves–Manning
  requires torsion-free; Dahmani–Guirardel–Osin cone radius ~5e12 vs injectivity <= 20; Coulon
  requires exponent > 100).
- Reusable machinery: the period-band method + certificate tooling, directly applicable to G5.
- Supporting theorems: G3 word-hyperbolic at FSA level (geodesic bigons 10-fellow-travel);
  orientation theorem; axis straightness for all powers; elementary-closure `E(p)=<p>`.
- Evidence layer (first night): E7 not a product of fifth powers with roots <= 15 (Stallings
  folding, ~1e9-edge computation, Rust/Python cross-validated).

   and its "all contact lengths" generality.

## What next (attacking infiniteness)

- **Second confirmation of G4:** let the independent automatic-structure build for G4 finish; if
  it completes it gives a second, tool-based proof of the same rung.
- **The actual open theorem:** a **uniform-in-L inductive step** ("E7 survives to G_L ⇒ survives
  to G_{L+1}" with L-independent constants — a per-level certified delta, or Dehn-filling
  constants uniform at n=5). Climbing rungs one-by-one is a treadmill; the uniform step is the
  only route that closes B(2,5).
