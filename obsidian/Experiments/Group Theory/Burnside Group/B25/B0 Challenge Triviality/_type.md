# B0 Challenge Triviality

Test whether given B(2,5) words are the identity in the **restricted** group B₀(2,5) = R(2,5) = B(2,5)/γ₁₃ (order 5^34, class 12), built with ANUPQ Pq in GAP. A pass is **necessary, not sufficient** for triviality in the free B(2,5) (see [[B25/_progress]] Sound/B0 split).
