# Structured Constructions (B(2,5))

**Build certified trivial words by explicit constructions instead of learned or random search.** Factors and conjugators are chosen from the structure of the relators: overlaps, diagrams, identities. Every construction emits a factor word (certificate), which the [[B25/Hardness Evaluation/_type|Hardness Evaluation]] harness then checks for L0, ρ_min16, D and level. Owner decision 2026-10-14: this type holds T-016 and, later, T-018 (diagram search) and T-019 (Hall–Petresco words). Source of the ideas: [[2026-10-14-cancellation-constructions]].
