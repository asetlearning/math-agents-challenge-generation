## What I confirmed
- **r10 and job 1 unchanged.** The diffs of the five modified files are additive: `max_free` defaults to 7 in `run_moves` and `a_r3` (r10), `label` defaults to None in `construct.build` and `depth_items`, and `pilot` changes only when `T028_STREAM_PREFIX` is set. `ar.attack` stays the r10 entry point.
- **N is a valid equality.** A step z = P u^k v Q -> P u^(k-5) v Q equals multiplying z on the left by P u^-5 P^-1; u^5 is a relator of G7 for |u| at most 2; the cell is logged with a literal core; rotations are conjugations; `replay` re-applies the log. The start of the run is the left-maximal one, u primitive, k = floor(run / |u|) from the run start, and the wrap case is rotated to the base point and logged (pins a of r11.1). Runs of period 1 and 2 only.
- **(ii-local) is sound.** The decomposition is checked letter by letter, both words are rotated to a common segment, each window loop alpha_j beta_j^-1 must reduce to IdWord under rec or slx, so N(w) and N(z) are equal in G7 up to conjugacy; with (i) (t' trivial under kbmag) F^5 invariance gives w trivial in B. Caps (64 windows, 120 s) end in "candidate kbmag failed", never a break. Recovery attribution bounds are as in the text (windows total at most |N(w)|/4, each at most 3 x the longest block, longest block at most |w|/8, |t'| at least 8).
- **A_R3 after N** runs on N(w) with `max_free = 0` (cells only from block-built periods, as the text says); a mismatch of the N replay stops (see R1).
- **Streams.** Control key "20261021|arctl|m|index"; fresh "20261022|stageC|arm|m|index" (prefix + arm in `pilot`, m and index in `label_seed`). The clash check covers all pilot W (CTRL included), the tune set, and the arctl words, up to rotation and inversion.
- **Reading rules.** `read_fresh`: clean needs arm C1, a stratum that passed the control (at least 50 % blind recoveries, n at least 10) and a residual length inside the control's range for that stratum; every C3 or C3r2 "no break" is "attack-limited (single-pair A_R)"; bounds are per assessed C1 stratum; ladder candidates are C1 only, clean, shortest first, spread over the passed strata, hashed. Fix: catch the exception per word and write an `error` row (counted as undetermined, never clean, never a break); `read` takes the expected ids (the verified T4u of the batch) and refuses to report a clean reading unless every id has a row and no row is an error; `summarize` counts `error` rows in the denominator and reports them.
