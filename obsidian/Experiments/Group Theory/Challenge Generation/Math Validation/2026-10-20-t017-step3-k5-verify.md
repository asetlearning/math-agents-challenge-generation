### H17 tally

The word min and the baseline are computed three ways:
- at k=5 alone;
- at k=4 alone (both objectives, Steps 1–2);
- pooled (k=4 both objectives + k=5), which is the right reading for "upper bound under M1": the minimum over every certificate found.

| class | flip vs flip (all three readings) | S2 vs S2, pooled = k=4 | S2 vs S2, k=5 alone |
|---|---|---|---|
| fails | ds547 | ds125, ds547, ds665 | ds125, ds547 |
| drops but stays above | ds94, ds125, ds160, ds512, ds566, ds665 | ds94, ds160, ds512, ds566 | ds94, ds160, ds512, ds566, **ds665** |
| unchanged | ds145, ds368, ds519, ds764, ds781 | the same 5 | the same 5 |

- **Flip classes: identical to k=4.** CONFIRMED.
- **S2 classes:** identical to k=4 when pooled. The k=5 depth_first run of ds665 ends at S2 3 (467 cells), while k=4 area_first reached S2 2. So `step3_sum.md` (k=5 alone) shows ds665 S2 as "drops-but-stays-above". That is correct for k=5 alone, but the pooled class stays **fails**.

### "No new depth drop": not quite

**Words.** No word's minimum drops below its k=4 minimum on either measure. At k=5 only N shrinks: for example ds125 goes from 242 to 241 cells, ds547 from 775 to 731, and ds160 from 318 to 302.

**Components.** Five components reach a lower minimum at k=5 than in either k=4 run:

| component | measure | k=4 min | k=5 min | k=5 certificate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ds160 parent** | S2 and flip (d) | 2 / 2 | **1 / 1** | 111 cells, fixpoint |
| ds125 tile00_k204 | flip (d) | 1 | 0 | 28 cells |
| ds160 tile01_k158 | S2 | 1 | 0 | 17 cells |
| ds519 L3 tile01_k607 | flip (d) | 1 | 0 | 34 cells |
| ds519 L3 tile39_k239 | S2 | 1 | 0 | 47 cells |

- Only the ds160 parent changes a baseline: **ds160's baseline drops from 2 / 2 to 1 / 1**. The word stays at 3 / 3, so it is still "drops but stays above", now by 2 levels.
- The tile drops do not change any baseline, because another component still holds the max.
- No class changes. A lower baseline can only move a word from "fails" towards "above". ds547's baseline is still 2, set by its parent at k=5 too.
