# T-028 r9: the R-aware recovery attack A_R

This is a Lead request (2026-10-20). It covers `MathExpert/proposals/2026-10-20-t028-pilot-preregistration-draft.md`, §7.0d and the [r9] edits to §3, §4, §7, §7.1, §8 and §9.6. Triage: [[2026-10-20-t028-preregistration-r9-ar-review-triage]]. This is a document review with my own derivations; I ran nothing.

**Notation.** w is the residual, cyclically reduced. φ: a ↦ x, b ↦ y is the generating pair of a C1 word W = φ(t); φ′ is a candidate pair (x′, y′). H = ⟨x′, y′⟩. F⁵ is the verbal subgroup generated by all fifth powers, so B = F/F⁵. A **U-cell** is a conjugate of u^{±5} for any u; an **R7 cell** has |u| ≤ 7.

## 1. The block-aligned lemma: correct, with one missing hypothesis

**The algebra checks.**
- R replaces a run s = u^p (p > 2.5) by c⁻¹ = u^{−(5−p)}, so r = s·c is a rotation of u⁵. (The rule u⁵ → empty is the case c = empty.)
- With W = P·s·Q, the new word is P·c⁻¹·Q = P·r⁻¹·P⁻¹·W: indeed P r⁻¹ P⁻¹ P s Q = P c⁻¹ s⁻¹ s Q = P c⁻¹ Q.
- If the run is block-aligned and P = φ(t_pre)·β₀, then r = β₀⁻¹·φ(v′)^{±5}·β₀, so P r⁻¹ P⁻¹ = φ(t_pre · v′^{∓5} · t_pre⁻¹), and the new word is φ(t₁) with t₁ = t_pre·v′^{∓5}·t_pre⁻¹·t, trivial in B. Same pair. Correct.
- R acts on a rotation of the cyclic word, and the rotation may start inside a block, so the statement is "freely conjugate to φ(t₁)". The text says that. By induction the consequence holds for any chain of block-aligned R steps.

**Fix 1: the hypothesis "the run's letter period is |φ(v)|".**
- R rewrites with the run's **minimal letter period** z. "The block sequence is periodic with period v" does not imply z = (rotation of) φ(v): φ(v) may be a proper power z^k as a letter word, because H need not be closed under roots. Example: x = a² and v = a (a power of a in t); then φ(v) = a² as letters, and R's base is a, not a².
- In that case r is a rotation of z⁵, which is in general not a φ-image, and the conclusion fails.
- **Fix:** add "and the minimal period of the run is |φ(v)| (equivalently R's base is a rotation of φ(v))". With v primitive and x, y a free basis this is the generic case, but the hypothesis must be stated.

**A remark on scope (no change needed).** The pool words t are 2.5-reduced (R does not change any of the 351 pool words, in my tcgraph check), so a block-aligned run with exponent > 2.5 in letters needs partial blocks at its ends. Block-aligned steps may therefore be a minority. Step 0 is the right way to find out.

**Pin 1: the flag must be on A_R1's conjugacy test, not on oracle success.**
- After block-aligned steps, t₁ contains v^{∓5} with |v| unrestricted. If |v| > 7, t₁ is trivial in B but not known to be trivial in G₇, and kbmag (criterion (i)) may fail on it.
- So an oracle failure on an all-block-aligned residual can come from kbmag, not from a failure of the lemma.
- **Pin:** the flag in item 5 reads "the oracle pair's **conjugacy-into-H test (A_R1)** fails on an R-only residual whose steps are all block-aligned". A failure of (i) after a successful A_R1 is reported separately ("membership found, kbmag failed on t′").
- Whether a step is block-aligned is decided from the R trace (the cells' cores and conjugators) together with x, y, which is oracle information. That is fine for the flag, which is oracle-only.

## 2. Success criteria (i) and (ii)

**(i) is sound.** kbmag's rec and slx rules are equalities in G₇ = ⟨a, b | v⁵, |v| ≤ 7⟩ (established in T-027). So t′ →* IdWord gives t′ ∈ ⟨⟨v⁵ : |v| ≤ 7⟩⟩ ⊆ F⁵. F⁵ is fully invariant, so φ′(t′) ∈ F⁵. Note that this holds for **any** endomorphism φ′, so x′, y′ need not be a basis of H, nor free. The text's "any basis of H works" is true but weaker than needed.

**(ii-Δ) is sound** for the same reason: Δ →* IdWord in G₇ gives Δ = 1 in B.

**(ii-exact) is sound:** U-cells are trivial in B, so rot_ρ(w) = φ′(t′)·∏cells⁻¹ = 1 in B.

**Fix 2: (ii) needs a conjugator, not only a rotation.**
- A_R1 finds a closed path labelled w at a vertex p of the core graph. If g is the label of a path from the base vertex to p, this gives **g·w·g⁻¹ = φ′(h)** for the element h of H read along the loop, so w is **conjugate** to φ′(t′), with t′ the word of h.
- Criterion (ii) asks for rot_ρ(w) = φ′(t′) as words (exact) or via Δ = rot_ρ(w)·φ′(t′)⁻¹. But φ′(t′), freely reduced, is g′·(rotation of w)·g′⁻¹ with g′ ≠ 1 whenever φ′(t′) is not cyclically reduced (for example x = a·b·a⁻¹ and y = a·b²·a⁻¹, where every φ′(t′) starts with a and ends with a⁻¹). Then no rotation of w is freely equal to φ′(t′), and Δ = rot(w)·g′·rot(w)⁻¹·g′⁻¹ is a commutator, which is not trivial in B in general. True recoveries would be rejected.
- §4 already says "freely equal to the segment **up to conjugation**". A_R lost that.
- **Fix:** A_R outputs (x′, y′, t′, g, cells) and (ii) reads g⁻¹·φ′(t′)·g = w·∏cells freely (exact), or Δ = w·(g⁻¹φ′(t′)g)⁻¹ →* IdWord (Δ route). Conjugation preserves triviality, so soundness is unchanged. ρ becomes a special case of g. The (ii-Δ) length condition |Δ| ≤ |w|/4 should be measured after this conjugation.

**A success is a valid break** once fix 2 is made: (i) + (ii) give w = g⁻¹φ′(t′)g·∏cells⁻¹ = 1 in B, an equality in B.

**Pin 2: "L0-certifiable later" means L0 over U, not over R7.**
- Mapping an R7 certificate of t′ through φ′ turns each cell c⁻¹v⁵c into φ′(c)⁻¹·φ′(v)⁵·φ′(c), a fifth power of a word of length |φ′(v)|, usually > 7. The A_R3 cells of type (b) (products of up to 3 blocks) are long too.
- So a certificate built this way is **L0 over U**. It becomes L0-cert (over R7) only after each long cell is certified over R7, and in my tcgraph re-check the PowerCellCertifier certified only 21 of 147 long R-trace cells (14 %) on C1-like words.
- **Pin:** write "L0-certifiable over U (scheme C), and over R7 only if the long cells are certified". This does not affect the break, which only needs B.

## 3. Stages A_R1–A_R4

- **A_R1 (whole-word conjugacy into H):** a cyclically reduced w is conjugate into H iff w labels a closed reduced path at some vertex of the core graph. A reduced closed path never enters the hair, so searching from every vertex of the folded graph is correct. Correct, with fix 2 for reading off g.
- **A_R2 (approximate matching):** it only proposes (x′, y′); acceptance is always (i) + (ii). Sound.
- **A_R3 (inverse R by cell insertion): sound by construction, yes.** Inserting r at position i of the current word w_k gives w_k[0:i]·r·w_k[i:] = w_k·(w_k[i:]⁻¹·r·w_k[i:]), a right multiplication by a U-cell. By induction w* = w·∏cells, with each cell explicit. A wrong guess only wastes budget. Two conditions make this exact:
  - every insertion is logged as an explicit cell (position, u, sign, rotation, as §9.6 says), and the final check (ii-exact) **re-expands** the logged cells against w by free reduction, independent of the search;
  - positions refer to the current (inserted, freely reduced) word, which the replay must follow in order.
- **A_R4 (the Δ route):** sound via (ii-Δ), with fix 2.

## 4. Nondegeneracy

- |x′|, |y′| ≥ 2, ⟨x′, y′⟩ ≠ F, and |t′| ≤ |w|/2: reasonable. By Schreier's formula a rank-2 subgroup of F₂ of finite index has index 1, so "≠ F" already means infinite index.
- **Pin 3 (small):** also require x′, y′ to be a **free basis** of a rank-2 subgroup (the folded graph has rank 2). If x′ and y′ are powers of one element, H is cyclic, and "recovering" w then only says that w is a power, which is degenerate. A_R2's Nielsen reduction detects this.
- The extra |Δ| ≤ |w|/4 for (ii-Δ): reasonable, measured after the conjugation of fix 2.
- Any verified success that fails the clause is still a break ("kbmag-dominated"): correct under the existence framing.

## 5. Budgets

- **Gated residuals:** 15 CPU-min (2 + 8 + 5), the same as the other held-out solvers; verification (b) at 30. Consistent. Note that one (ii-Δ) attempt can cost up to 2 × 60 CPU-s (both systems), so A_R4's 5 minutes allow only about 2 Δ attempts after the parse. That is a design choice, but the report should give the number of Δ attempts.
- **Pin 4: the control's ceiling and the 4 CPU-h pause.**
  - Control set: up to 150 C1 residuals + 80 R-depth residuals = 230, × 2 modes × 3 CPU-min = **about 23 CPU-h at most**.
  - Successes are cheap and failures run to the cap. So the total exceeds 4 CPU-h as soon as about 80 runs fail, that is when the combined success rate is below about 65 %. Blind failures on D-containing residuals are expected (§7.0d.5), so the pause is likely in exactly the informative case.
  - The pause is the right safety, but r9 should say what happens then: for example, finish oracle mode on all residuals first, then blind mode in index order, and stop blind at the cap with the rate on the residuals done. Otherwise the 50 % rule may lack its denominator.
- **Budget mismatch (state it):** the control runs at 3 CPU-min, the gated residuals at 15. The 50 % rule then judges the 15-minute attack by its 3-minute rate, which understates its strength, so more C3 failures are labelled "attack-limited" than warranted. The label is interpretive only, so this is acceptable if the rule says "blind success at the control budget".

## 6. The positive control and the 50 % rule

- The set (all C1 residuals plus an R-depth series of 4 W per cell, R stopped after 1, 2, 4, 8 steps) and the two modes are well chosen. The R-depth series isolates R from KB and D.
- **Denominators:** state that residuals R or the chain emptied are excluded (A_R has nothing to recover), and that residuals equal to W (chain did nothing) are reported separately, since §4 already covers them.
- **Pin 5: the rule per stratum.** The expectation is that D-step residuals are not recoverable. A pooled C1 rate then depends on the chain mix, which differs between C1 and C3. Apply the 50 % rule **per chain-composition stratum** (R-only, contains KB, contains D): a C3 failure is "attack-limited" if blind A_R succeeds on < 50 % of the C1 residuals **of the same stratum**. Keep the pooled rate as reported.
- The interpretive-only label, the tiers unchanged, and the lemma flag going to Lead and the Math Expert: correct.

## 7. Held-out discipline

- Freezing the parameters and repo@sha before job 1, a separate tune set, oracle mode never producing a survivor verdict, no feedback into generation: correct.
- **The tune set and the calibration residual.** The tune labels "20261020|tune|m|index" give W disjoint from the pilot's labels "20261020|C1|…". But the 849-letter residual came from the Developer's **calibration** W. If calibration used a pilot label, that W **is** a pilot word (generation is deterministic), and its residual would enter the pilot's control set and its gated set.
  - **Pin:** record the calibration W's label. If it is a pilot label, exclude that index from the control rates **and** mark that word "tuned on" in the A_R verdicts. Also check that no tune W equals a pilot W (exact comparison; one line).
- **What blind mode reads.** The list (not W, x, y, the certificate or the reproduction block) misses three generation inputs:
  - the **t-pool** (351 words): t is one of them, so the pool narrows t′ to a lookup;
  - the **arm and m** (cell membership), which fix the block-length ranges of x and y;
  - the R trace and chain composition, which are in the reproduction block (covered, but say so, since the stratification uses them).

  **Pin:** blind mode reads only w and the fixed public tools (the R7 table and the kbmag systems). The block-length range 2–40 is generic and fine.

## Verdict

**CONFIRMED on soundness:**
- (i) + (ii) prove w = 1 in B, so a verified success is a valid break;
- A_R2 only proposes;
- A_R3 is sound by construction (right multiplication by logged U-cells, re-expanded at acceptance);
- the lemma's algebra is right.

**Before r9 is frozen, two text fixes:**
1. (ii) with a logged conjugator g, not only a rotation (otherwise true recoveries fail);
2. the lemma's hypothesis that the run's minimal letter period is |φ(v)|.

**And five pins:**
1. the lemma flag on A_R1's conjugacy test, not on overall oracle success;
2. "L0-certifiable" means over U (scheme C images and type (b) cells are long);
3. a free rank-2 basis in the nondegeneracy clause;
4. the control's ceiling (about 23 CPU-h): an order of runs for when the 4 CPU-h pause fires, and the 50 % rule stated at the control budget;
5. the 50 % rule per chain-composition stratum; plus the held-out items: the calibration W's label, and blind mode not reading the t-pool, the arm or m.

None needs a new mathematical decision; fix 1 and pin 2 are wording with consequences for what is recorded.
