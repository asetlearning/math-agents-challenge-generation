## Claim 1 — ker(B→R) = γ₁₃(B) = γω(B); B infinite ⟺ γ₁₃(B)≠1

**VERDICT: #status/proven.** I reconstructed the argument independently; it is airtight:

- For every n: B/γ_n(B) is 2-gen, exp 5, nilpotent (class ≤ n−1) ⇒ f.g. nilpotent torsion ⇒
  **finite** (elementary: f.g. nilpotent torsion = finite) ⇒ a quotient of R (universal property
  of the restricted Burnside group).
- class(R)=12 (GAP, 3 ways: γ₁₃(R)=1, all 8192 weight-13 left-normed commutators trivial, [γ₁₂,R]=1;
  + HWW 1974) ⇒ γ₁₃(R)=1 ⇒ γ₁₃(B) ⊆ ker(B→R).
- Two surjections between finite groups: R ↠ B/γ₁₃(B) (B/γ₁₃ is a quotient of R) and B/γ₁₃(B) ↠ R
  (γ₁₃(B)⊆ker) ⇒ |B/γ₁₃|=|R|=5³⁴ ⇒ both are isomorphisms ⇒ **ker(B→R)=γ₁₃(B)**.
- Same with γ_n, n≥13: B/γ_n is a quotient of R that surjects onto B/γ₁₃=R ⇒ order 5³⁴ ⇒
  γ_n(B)=γ₁₃(B) ∀n≥13 ⇒ **γ₁₃(B)=γω(B)**, and γ₁₃=[γ₁₃,B] (B-perfect core). ✓

**Citation refinement (the human's explicit question): Kostrikin suffices; full Zelmanov is NOT
needed.** The restricted Burnside problem for *prime* exponent was solved by Kostrikin (1959); p=5
is prime, so R(2,5) finite + the universal property follow from Kostrikin alone. Zelmanov (1989–91)
is only needed for prime-*power* exponent. The memo's "Zelmanov/RBP" should read "Kostrikin 1959."

## Claim 2 — E7=[a,6b] Engel witness; no finite certificate

**VERDICT: #status/proven** for the logic + all computational facts (E7=1 in R; E7≠1 ⇒ B infinite;
6-Engel is a law ⇒ Trap 5). GAP confirms `e7_witness.txt` **is** [a,6b] as a free-group word (138
chars) and → 1 in R; [a,5b]≠1 (so B not 5-Engel, E7 is the minimal separating Engel word); and
[x,6y]=1 is a genuine 2-variable **law** in R (0/3000 random-pair counterexamples), hence — by
relative-freeness (R = F₂ of the variety generated by finite exp-5 groups; a 2-var law holds in the
variety iff it holds on the 2 free generators) — **every finite exp-5 group is 6-Engel**. Therefore
no finite/nilpotent quotient can ever certify E7≠1 (Trap 5), matching residual-finiteness blindness.

**Non-circularity (I specifically checked this against the historical 119-word circularity trap):**
E7 is a **sufficient, not necessary** witness. E7≠1 in B ⇒ B infinite is valid and non-circular
(contrapositive: B finite ⇒ B=R ⇒ 6-Engel ⇒ E7=1). The converse fails: B infinite does NOT force
E7≠1. The memo states this asymmetry correctly (Trap 5 / line 89: even E7=1 would not prove B
finite — that needs "6-Engel+exp5 ⇒ locally finite", OPEN; only 4-Engel proven, Vaughan-Lee 1997).
Unlike the 119 benchmark words (trivial-in-B0 by construction, used as a proxy score — circular),
E7's triviality is in R and the infiniteness implication is a real, one-directional theorem.

## Claim 3 — Stallings H_M membership certificate

**VERDICT: #status/proven** (code correct, semantics exact, result reproduced).

- **Semantics.** ⟨⟨fifth powers⟩⟩ = ⟨fifth powers⟩ as a subgroup, because the set {d⁵} is
  inverse-closed ((d⁵)⁻¹=(d⁻¹)⁵) and conjugation-closed (g d⁵ g⁻¹=(gdg⁻¹)⁵). So "=1 in B(2,5)" ⟺
  "is a product of fifth powers." H_M=⟨d⁵:|d|≤M⟩ truncates roots; membership in H_M is **exactly**
  "a product of fifth powers whose roots have length ≤ M" (any factor count). H_M ↑ F⁵ as M→∞. ✓
- **fold.py audited + known-answer tested.** Stallings folding (union-find, fold to fixpoint,
  accept = closed loop at base) — standard and correct. `gen_reduced_words` includes ALL reduced
  roots incl. non-cyclically-reduced (verified: `abA` etc. are generated) — required for the exact
  certificate; the proper-power skip is valid (d=fᵏ ⇒ d⁵∈⟨f⁵⟩, f already present). My 8 known-answer
  cases all pass, incl. the **decisive bounded-M discriminator** `b·a⁵·B` (root `baB`, len 3):
  NON-member at M=2, member at M=3. Abelianization obstructions (`a`, `a⁴`) and the genuine
  non-member `[a,b]` (≠1 in B) resolve correctly.
- **Result reproduced.** I re-ran `fold.py 6 real_targets.txt`: E7 and all 20 W13/W14 candidates
  give MEMBER=False (21/21). Consistent with their out_M10/out_M11 receipts (∉H_10, ∉H_11).
- **Discipline (correctly stated by claimant):** ∉H_M is an **obstruction frontier**, evidence
  *toward* E7∉F⁵ (⟺ E7≠1 ⟺ B infinite) but **never a proof**. Must not be over-claimed downstream.
